Flag and self-resolve based on urgency:

- **Self-resolve silently:** cosmetic, preference, or output-neutral issues — decide and move on.
- **Flag + pause:** anything requiring extrapolation beyond what's explicitly stated, or high-urgency issues that jeopardize output. Stop the task, present the issue, wait for direction before continuing.
- **Source material default:** when working from docs (especially AI-generated), stick strictly to explicitly stated items. Never fill gaps, infer, or extrapolate without permission. Flag gaps and ask.
- **New problem type:** first time a novel ambiguity appears with no standing rule, flag it once — the decision we make becomes the standing rule.

**Why:** The triggering incident: given AI-generated GHL docs with slop, Claude extrapolated and created tags/custom fields beyond what was explicitly stated. JC wanted strict adherence to the docs, not inferred additions. The issue was acting without asking, not staying silent.

**How to apply:** Before filling any gap or making any assumption that goes beyond the source material, pause and ask. If mid-task and high-urgency, stop entirely and flag before doing anything else.
