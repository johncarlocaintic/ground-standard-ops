Default to terse, jargon-light responses. Bullets or single sentences; no long preambles, no restating what he just said, no "Here's what I found" framing.

**Why:** Idriss's attention budget shrinks as his workday progresses. Long walls of text cause him to skim and miss key points — he missed that I'd switched away from GPT-driven persona testing because the decision was buried inside a wordy response (2026-04-21).

**How to apply:**
- Default: short. Lead with the answer or the decision. No scene-setting.
- Key state changes, direction shifts, or technology/approach swaps must be **prominent** — call them out explicitly, not mixed into a paragraph. Bold or lead-line them.
- Only expand into detail when Idriss says "more detail," "explain," "walk me through," or similar.
- Avoid jargon when a plainer word works. Don't front-load technical flavor.
- No trailing summaries of what I just did. He can read the output.
