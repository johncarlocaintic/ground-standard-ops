================================================================================
CLASS DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

FOUNDATIONS - Structured for beginners and those newer to jiu-jitsu. Develops the fundamental movement patterns and positional understanding that form the basis of all training at Logica. The right starting point for new students. Multiple slots run through the week.

ALL LEVELS - For intermediate practitioners with a working familiarity with jiu-jitsu, ready to train alongside a broader range of partners and skill levels.

TAKEDOWNS - Standup range: clinch work, entries, and takedown mechanics for no-gi grappling. Mondays at 6:00 PM.

OPEN MAT - Unstructured training time to roll freely with partners. No instruction is provided. Fridays at noon and 5:30 PM, Saturdays 11:00 AM-12:30 PM.

KIDS PROGRAM - Ages 8-13. Develops movement, coordination, and grappling fundamentals in a structured, age-appropriate environment. Monday/Wednesday/Friday 5:00-6:00 PM.

================================================================================
TRAINING METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Logica uses the Constraints-Led Approach (CLA): a research-backed coaching framework that develops skill through structured, live problem-solving within defined constraints rather than isolated repetition. Practitioners build adaptive, responsive movement through guided exploration and real-time decision-making. The CLA is used in high-performance sports environments globally. Logica is a no-gi academy with a non-traditional training atmosphere.
