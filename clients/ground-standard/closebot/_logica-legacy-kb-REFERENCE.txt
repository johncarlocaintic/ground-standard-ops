CLASS DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

FOUNDATIONS
Foundations classes are structured for beginners and those newer to jiu-jitsu. These sessions develop the fundamental movement patterns and positional understanding that form the basis of all training at Logica. Multiple Foundations time slots are available throughout the week to fit different schedules.

ALL LEVELS
All Levels classes are open to intermediate practitioners who have developed a working familiarity with jiu-jitsu. These sessions are appropriate for students ready to train alongside a broader range of partners at varying skill levels.

TAKEDOWNS
The Takedowns class addresses standup range — clinch work, entries, and takedown mechanics applicable to nogi grappling. This class runs on Mondays at 6:00 PM.

OPEN MAT
Open Mat provides unstructured training time for practitioners to apply their skills freely with partners. No instruction is provided during Open Mat sessions. Open Mat is available Fridays at noon and 5:30 PM, and Saturdays from 11:00 AM to 12:30 PM.

KIDS PROGRAM
The Kids Program is for students ages 8-13. Classes meet Monday, Wednesday, and Friday from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM. The program is designed to develop movement, coordination, and grappling fundamentals in a structured and age-appropriate environment.

================================================================================
TRAINING METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Logica Jiu-Jitsu uses the Constraints-Led Approach (CLA) to training. The CLA is a research-backed coaching framework that develops skill through structured, live problem-solving rather than isolated repetition. Practitioners develop adaptive, responsive movement by working within defined constraints that guide exploration and decision-making in real time. The CLA is used across high-performance sports environments globally.

Logica is a nogi academy. All training is conducted without a gi. The academy maintains a non-traditional atmosphere.
