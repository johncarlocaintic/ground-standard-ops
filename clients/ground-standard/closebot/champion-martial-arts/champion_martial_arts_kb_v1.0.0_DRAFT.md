## SECTION 1 — BUSINESS INFORMATION


Full Business Name: Champion Martial Arts
Also Known As: Champion Martial Arts and Fitness
Industry: Martial Arts — Judo, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Karate
Location: Pasadena, TX

Description: Champion Martial Arts is a martial arts academy in Pasadena, Texas
with over 30 years of operation and thousands of students trained. The academy
offers Judo, Carlson Gracie Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and Shotokan Karate across
recreational and competitive tracks. The academy is affiliated with the
Silverback Fight Team. The academy's guiding philosophy is "Be A Champion" —
helping every student discover their inner champion on the mat and in life
through self-defense, discipline, fitness, and competition.

Programs Offered:
- Judo (USA Judo registered instructors)
- Carlson Gracie Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (Ages 12+)
- Shotokan Karate (Ages 5+)
- Champion Conditioning
- BJJ Open Mat / Technique Review

Age Restrictions:
- Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu: Ages 12 and up only
- Judo and Karate: Available for ages 5 and up

Classes are divided between recreational and competitive tracks. Up to 10 black
belts are available across sessions to provide small-group and semi-private
attention within regular classes. Student-to-instructor ratio is 12:1.

## SECTION 8 — POLICIES


Sign-Up Process:
New students sign up by calling, texting, or emailing the academy. Enrollment
begins with a one-week free trial.

Uniforms:
Standard BJJ or Judo gi is required. Loaner uniforms are available for the
trial week at no charge and can be purchased at the dojo or brought from
outside. Athletic or yoga-style clothing is worn underneath the uniform.

Belt Promotions:
Belt promotions are based on performance and attendance, reviewed approximately
every three months at instructor discretion. Rank testing fee applies (amount
confirmed with staff). Daily attendance is logged for rank eligibility for
children's classes.

Student-to-Instructor Ratio: 12:1.

Parental Viewing: Parents may observe all classes.

Guest Policy: Guests may attend a class under the trial program or observe.

Women in Classes: No dedicated women's-only class. Partner preferences are
respected during training.

Special Needs and Allergy Accommodations: Available, with certified staff on-site.

Bullying and Harassment: Zero tolerance policy, with strict adherence to USA
Judo's written guidelines.

Photography: No restrictions.

Birthday Parties and Private Events: Not offered.

Online Classes: Not available.

Online Study Materials: Available for technique reinforcement and review.

Community Outreach: Special pricing available for veterans, military members,
and first responders.
