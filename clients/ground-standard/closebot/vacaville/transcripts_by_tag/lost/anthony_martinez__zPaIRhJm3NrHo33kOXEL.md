Mindfulness is a practice that has its origins in Eastern religion, but it doesn’t have to be a religious practice. This is a
simple definition of mindfulness:

A state of active, open attention to the present moment. A person who is mindful can observe their thoughts and feelings without
judgment.

Another way of thinking about it is that mindfulness is emotional detachment. That doesn’t mean you don’t feel emotions. Instead,
it means that you learn that your emotions are part of you, but they don’t have to govern your behavior. You can feel them without
having to be ruled by them.

It’s common for people to let their minds wander. Your partner doesn’t put the dishes away and suddenly, you find yourself yelling
at them about something they did last week… or last year. Or you get upset and lash out at someone you love instead of taking a
moment to consider the effect your words might have.

The practice of mindfulness teaches us to let go of worry and anxiety. One way to build mindfulness is to meditate. Another is to
study martial arts.
