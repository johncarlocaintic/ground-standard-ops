Coach Nick from Vacaville Grappling Academy again. Today, I want to tell you about why taking one of our Adult MA classes can help
you shed excess weight.

Let’s face it, as we get older, it gets more difficult to lose weight. Our metabolisms get slower. Also, many adults have
sedentary jobs. We’re not getting the kind of incidental exercise that we would if we were on our feet all day or doing physical
labor.

That’s why it’s important to get our bodies moving regularly. Fitness guidelines usually recommend 30 to 60 minutes of
cardiovascular exercise a minimum of three times a week. Without exercise, we pack on pounds because we’re simply not burning
enough calories.

Speaking of calories, that’s really what it boils down to. If you take in more calories than you burn, you’ll gain weight. If you
burn more than you consume, you’ll lose weight. Our martial arts classes are great for burning calories. These are intense
workouts that require a lot of energy!

The other weight loss element of studying martial arts is building muscle. You see, muscle takes up less space than fat and it
weighs more than fat. But, increasing your muscle mass also helps your body burn calories. It requires more energy to maintain a
pound of muscle than it does a pound of fat.

I’ve seen people come into Vacaville Grappling Academy who wanted to lose a few pounds and some who needed to lose a lot of
