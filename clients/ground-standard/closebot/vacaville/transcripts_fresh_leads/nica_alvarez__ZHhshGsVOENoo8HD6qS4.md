Discipline is a core tenet of martial arts because it requires discipline to learn skills, master them, and earn new belts and

The thing is, that’s true of adults too. Some of us can discipline ourselves in one area but not in another. For example, someone

the other.

carry over into other areas of your life. You’ll understand that the decisions you make today will affect the quality of your life
