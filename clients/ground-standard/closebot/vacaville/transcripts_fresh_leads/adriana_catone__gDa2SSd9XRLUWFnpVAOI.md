One thing that many of us worry about as we get older is getting injured. There are all kinds of injuries that can slow us down,
take away our mobility, and make it more difficult for us to enjoy our lives. And back injuries can be horrible to deal with.

So… what if I told you that studying martial arts can help protect and strengthen your back, making it less likely that you’ll
injure it or end up with a problem?

It’s true. Martial arts workouts strengthen your whole body. In particular, they do a great job of strengthening your core
muscles. These are the muscles in the middle of your body, and they include your pelvic floor muscles, abdominals, lateral and
back muscles, and even your gluteus maximus.

When these core muscles are fit and strong, they stabilize your back. They help you to stand and sit straight, and they provide
your back with the support it needs.

Does this mean you’ll never get a back injury? Well, we can’t promise that – we’re all different, and accidents happen. What we
can tell you, though, is that you’ll be less likely to tweak your back when the muscles that support it are strong.

What if you’ve experienced back injuries in the past or even had back surgery? You might be wondering if it’s safe for you to take
martial arts classes. That’s an important issue, so let’s talk about it.

The first thing you should know is that if you’ve had serious back issues, you should talk to your doctor before signing up with
