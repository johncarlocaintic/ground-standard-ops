Let’s face it, as we get older, it gets more difficult to lose weight. Our metabolisms get slower. Also, many adults have

Speaking of calories, that’s really what it boils down to. If you take in more calories than you burn, you’ll gain weight. If you

weighs more than fat. But, increasing your muscle mass also helps your body burn calories. It requires more energy to maintain a
pound of muscle than it does a pound of fat.

I’ve seen people come into Vacaville Grappling Academy who wanted to lose a few pounds and some who needed to lose a lot of

Lawson,

You know now that taking martial arts classes can strengthen your heart, but you might be wondering what effect it will have on
your blood pressure. High blood pressure, or hypertension, is something that affects many of us.

I have good news for you. There’s plenty of evidence to support the idea that getting regular cardiovascular workouts lowers your
blood pressure. That’s because working out strengthens your heart.

Your heart is a pump. Its job is to circulate your blood. When your heart is weak, as it is if you don’t get enough exercise, it
has to work really hard to do its job. But, when you strengthen your heart with exercise, it works more efficiently – pumping more
blood with less effort.

That efficiency reduces the strain on your heart and lowers your blood pressure. Sometimes, people who rely on medication to keep
their blood pressure in control find that they can stop taking it after they begin classes with us.

Of course, I can’t promise that the same will happen for you – individual results can vary. But what I can tell you is that
martial arts classes will strengthen your heart and improve your circulation. That’s good news no matter how you slice it!

Having high blood pressure is a risk factor for many serious health problems including heart attacks and strokes. If you combine
regular martial arts workouts with a healthy diet, you’ll improve your blood pressure and protect your heart. Those are both great
things!
