Hi William,

At times, we all struggle with self-control. We might get upset when our kids squabble or misbehave. A coworker drops the ball and
we get angry and say something we regret. It happens to everyone.

The thing is, losing control is rarely productive. It almost always hurts us. It can lead to feelings of guilt and shame, and it
can also negatively impact our relationships with the people around us. That’s not want you want – and the good news is that
spending time in a martial arts classroom can help.

While you might think that a sport that involves sparring and fighting doesn’t encourage self-control, the opposite is true. To
become a skilled martial artist, you must learn and practice self-control at all times. It’s the only thing that will lead to
success.

Self-control goes with focus and discipline. It’s impossible to be focused if you’re out of control. A lack of control makes
discipline go out the window, too. It’s inevitable.

The reason that studying martial arts helps with self-control is that every move you make in class or in competition must be
controlled. We teach our students to focus on form and intention. A deliberate, controlled motion is always better than a wild,
uncontrolled one.

As you master the art of self-control on the mat, you’ll find that it becomes natural to exercise the same restraint in other
areas of your life. Instead of lashing out in anger, you’ll find that you can take a step back and think about what you say before
you say it, instead of regretting it after the fact.

In other words, the self-control you practice with us will lead to greater self-control in every situation. You’ll be less likely
to overreact, and more likely to take the measure of a situation. That’s an important life skill and one that will serve you well.
