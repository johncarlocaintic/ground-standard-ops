## Mission & positioning

The academy describes itself as "the only dedicated No-Gi Jiu Jitsu and submission grappling academy in Solano County." Their stated mission focuses on creating "a training environment that is challenging, inclusive, and focused on long-term development — both on and off the mat."

## Distinguishing approach

The facility employs the **Constraint-Led Approach**, emphasizing "adaptability, self-organization, and real-time decision making" rather than rigid technique patterns. This methodology prioritizes "live resistance and movement problem-solving."
