## Business summary

Vacaville Grappling Academy offers martial arts instruction in No-Gi Jiu Jitsu, submission grappling, and wrestling for both youth (ages 7–13) and adults. The academy emphasizes practical technique development through live-resistance training rather than flashy moves. Programs focus on building physical fitness, self-defense capabilities, mental resilience, and confidence. The facility is located in Vacaville, California and welcomes students at all skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced competitors.
