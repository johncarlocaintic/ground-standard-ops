ABOUT THE GYM
================================================================================

Hamptons Jiu-Jitsu is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and martial arts academy serving the Hamptons area of Long Island, New York. The academy operates two locations: Southampton (headquarters) and Westhampton Beach.

The academy was founded by Greg Melita with the philosophy of offering multiple teaching styles and lineages under one roof, rather than the traditional single-team model. This gives students exposure to a wider range of techniques and approaches.

Hamptons Jiu-Jitsu runs the largest youth martial arts program in the Hamptons.

ADULT PROGRAMS
================================================================================

ADULT BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU — FUNDAMENTALS
A structured beginner program for adults with little or no prior martial arts experience. Covers the foundational techniques and concepts of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in a welcoming, judgment-free environment. Suitable for adults starting from scratch.

ADULT BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU — ALL LEVELS
Open to adults at all skill levels. Classes integrate practitioners from beginner through advanced, allowing newer students to train alongside more experienced training partners. Covers Gi and No-Gi BJJ.

ADULT BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU — ADVANCED
For established members with a solid foundation in BJJ. This class is for continuing students currently enrolled at the academy.

ADULT MUAY THAI & MMA
The academy offers Muay Thai, Judo, Wrestling, and MMA classes for adults. These programs are available for fitness, self-defense, and competition training.

ADULT TRX FITNESS
Hamptons Powered by TRX is a functional fitness program that complements the martial arts training. Uses suspension strap and gravity-based resistance training. Classes are led by certified TRX instructors.

================================================================================
YOUTH PROGRAMS
================================================================================

YOUTH BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU (AGES 3–5)
An introductory martial arts class for young children ages 3 to 5. Focuses on movement, coordination, listening skills, and basic mat etiquette in a fun and age-appropriate setting.

YOUTH BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU (AGES 6–9)
Jiu-Jitsu classes for children ages 6 to 9. Builds on foundational movement with technique instruction, partner drilling, and character development.

YOUTH BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU (AGES 10–14)
Jiu-Jitsu classes for youth ages 10 to 14. More structured and technique-focused, preparing students for the full range of BJJ curriculum.

The youth martial arts program at Hamptons Jiu-Jitsu is the largest in the Hamptons. The program emphasizes building confidence, discipline, and focus in young students alongside the physical curriculum.

GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

New students are encouraged to try a free introductory class. No prior experience is required. Beginners start in the Fundamentals program and progress at their own pace.

What to bring to your first class:
- Comfortable athletic clothing that allows for movement (shorts and a t-shirt are fine)
- Water bottle
- Open mind

New students are advised to arrive a few minutes early to meet the instructors and get oriented before class begins. The staff works with beginners to make the first experience as comfortable as possible.

No Gi attire (rash guard, board shorts, spats) is acceptable for No-Gi classes. A Gi (uniform) is required for Gi classes. Instructors can advise on gear after the first visit.

Hygiene standards: all students are expected to shower before and after class and keep nails trimmed. Training gear (Gi, rash guards) should be washed after every session.

================================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Do I need any experience to start?
No prior martial arts experience is required. The Fundamentals program is specifically designed for adults starting from zero. Beginners are welcomed at every class.

Q: Is BJJ safe?
Yes. Tapping out (tapping your partner multiple times) is used to signal that a technique should be released. No striking is allowed in BJJ classes. Instructors supervise technique to keep training safe.

Q: Can my child train even if they have no martial arts background?
Yes. The youth programs start from the very basics. Kids with zero experience train alongside others in structured, age-appropriate classes.

Q: What is the difference between Gi and No-Gi?
Gi BJJ is practiced in a traditional uniform (the Gi) with gripping the jacket and pants as part of the technique. No-Gi uses shorts and rash guards with no grips on clothing. The academy offers both.

Q: Does the gym train competitors?
Yes. The gym trains students for fitness, self-defense, and competition. Muay Thai, Wrestling, Judo, and MMA classes are available for those pursuing competition training.

Q: How do I get more information or sign up?
Reach out via the website at hamptonsjiujitsu.com or visit the gym in person. The team is happy to answer questions about programs, scheduling, and getting started.
