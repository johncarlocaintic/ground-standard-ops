INCONSISTENCIES IDENTIFIED FROM SOURCE MATERIAL
================================================================================

The following inconsistencies were found during content review and should be
clarified with the client before deployment:

INCONSISTENCY 1 — Wednesday 6:15 PM Class Is No-Gi, Not Gi
The full class schedule notes that Wednesday's 6:15 PM class is "Beginner Gi
(No-Gi on Wed)" — meaning Wednesday beginners attend a No-Gi class, not Gi.
However, the Introductory Class Schedule simply lists "Monday through Thursday
@ 6:15 PM" without flagging this distinction. A prospect registering for their
trial on a Wednesday expecting a Gi class would be misinformed. Recommend
client confirm and clarify Wednesday intro class type in all public-facing
materials. This KB reflects the schedule detail (Wednesday = No-Gi).

INCONSISTENCY 2 — "One Free Trial Class" vs. "2 Free Weeks at Sign-Up"
The document states two separate offers: (1) one free trial class for
prospective students before joining, and (2) two free weeks given to new
members at sign-up. These are distinct offers at different stages of the
funnel, but they are listed together without clear separation, which could
cause confusion. This KB treats them as two separate offers and labels them
accordingly. Client should confirm both offers are current and active.

INCONSISTENCY 3 — Kids Competition Class vs. Kids Intro Class
The 5:00 PM Monday "Kids Comp Class" is a competition-track class, not a
general or introductory session. The introductory schedule for kids lists
Monday–Thursday at 5:30 PM and Saturdays at 9:30 AM. The 5 PM Comp class
is not an appropriate intro option and has been excluded from the trial class
schedule in this KB. Client should confirm this is correct.

INCONSISTENCY 4 — Drop-In Fee Not Specified
The document confirms drop-ins are allowed but provides no pricing. This KB
notes that drop-in pricing is available by contacting the school directly.
Client should provide a specific drop-in rate to improve bot answer quality.

INTRODUCTORY / TRIAL CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

TRIAL CLASS POLICY:
Montgomery BJJ offers one free introductory/trial class for all prospective
students. No commitment or obligation is required to attend the trial class.

WHAT TO EXPECT:
Introductory classes are held during regularly scheduled beginner classes or
the 11:00 AM All Levels No-Gi classes. There is no separate time slot reserved
exclusively for trial students — new students join existing scheduled classes.

GI PROVISION:
A gi (uniform) is provided by the school for introductory Gi classes. Students
attending a No-Gi trial class do not need a gi — traditional gym attire is
appropriate.

ARRIVAL REQUIREMENT:
New students are asked to arrive 10 to 15 minutes before class start time to
complete any required intake steps before joining the mat.

WHAT TO WEAR:
• For Gi classes: A gi is provided. Wear comfortable athletic clothing underneath.
• For No-Gi classes: Traditional gym attire (shorts, rash guard, or athletic
  t-shirt) is appropriate.
• Water bottles are recommended. Water is available on-site.

PROGRAMS OFFERED
================================================================================

BRAZILIAN JIU JITSU — GI
Traditional BJJ training using the gi (uniform). Beginner classes are offered
Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday evenings at 6:15 PM. The Gi format emphasizes
grips, control, and technical application using the uniform.

BRAZILIAN JIU JITSU — NO-GI
No-Gi training removes the uniform, with students wearing shorts and rash
guards or athletic gear. Classes are available across multiple time slots
throughout the week including morning and evening sessions.

KIDS PROGRAM
BJJ training for children in a safe, structured environment focused on
self-defense skills and bully-proofing. Both Gi and No-Gi kids classes are
available. A competition-track Kids Comp Class is available on Monday evenings
at 5:00 PM for students pursuing competitive training.

SPECIALTY CLASSES (Adults):
• Leg Locks — Monday evenings at 7:00 PM. Focused submission training.
• ADCC No-Gi — Tuesday and Thursday evenings at 7:00 PM. No-Gi grappling
  following ADCC ruleset.

OPEN MAT:
Friday evenings at 5:30 PM. Open mat allows students to roll and drill freely
without structured instruction. Typically available to current members.

Q: Do you offer a free trial class?
A: Yes. Montgomery BJJ offers one free introductory class for all prospective
students. No commitment is required. Trial students join a regularly scheduled
beginner or all-levels class — there is no separate trial-only session. For Gi
classes, a gi is provided at no charge.

Q: When can I take my free trial class?
A: Adults can attend a trial during the 6:15 PM beginner classes Monday through
Thursday (note: Wednesday's 6:15 PM session is No-Gi while Mon/Tue/Thu are Gi),
or at the 11:00 AM No-Gi classes on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, or Saturday
at 10:30 AM. Kids trial classes are available Monday through Thursday at
5:30 PM, and Saturdays at 9:30 AM.

Q: What should I wear to my first class?
A: For Gi trial classes, a gi is provided — wear comfortable athletic clothing
underneath. For No-Gi classes, standard gym attire such as shorts and a t-shirt
or rash guard works well. No special equipment is needed for the trial.

discuss all the pricing options and membership plans with you during or right

for you and provide accurate pricing for your situation. The trial class is
completely free with no commitment required.

Q: Is there any special offer for new members?

Q: Do you offer classes for kids?
A: Yes. Kids classes are available Monday through Thursday at 5:30 PM and

Q: What is the school's address?
A: Montgomery BJJ is located at 21 Belle Mead Griggstown Road, Montgomery,

Q: What are your business hours?
A: On Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, classes run from 11:00 AM to 12:30 PM and
again in the evenings from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Tuesday and Thursday have an
early morning session from 6:00 AM to 7:00 AM and an evening session from
5:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Friday evenings wind down with Open Mat starting at
5:30 PM. Saturday hours are 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM. The school is closed on
Sundays.

Q: What is Open Mat?
A: Open Mat is held on Friday evenings at 5:30 PM. It is an unstructured
session where students roll and drill at their own pace without formal
instruction.

Q: What is the difference between Gi and No-Gi BJJ?
A: Gi BJJ is trained wearing the traditional uniform (gi), which allows for
grip-based techniques using the fabric. No-Gi BJJ is trained in athletic shorts
and a rash guard without the uniform, and focuses on body-based grappling
without gi grips. Montgomery BJJ offers both formats across the weekly schedule.

Q: What are the Leg Locks and ADCC No-Gi classes?
A: These are specialty adult classes. Leg Locks (Mondays at 7:00 PM) focus
specifically on lower-body submission techniques. ADCC No-Gi (Tuesdays and
Thursdays at 7:00 PM) follows the ADCC ruleset, which is one of the most
prestigious formats in competitive submission grappling.

Q: Do I need any experience to join?
A: No experience is required. Beginner classes are specifically designed to
welcome students at all levels, and the 11:00 AM All Levels No-Gi and Saturday
10:30 AM classes are also open to newcomers.

Q: Who is the head instructor?
A: Miguel Benitez is the owner and head instructor of Montgomery BJJ. He is a

experience to the mats. Miguel was recently named Head Referee for ADCC
America and has officiated for ONE FC in Thailand.

Q: How long has Montgomery BJJ been open?
A: Montgomery BJJ has been serving the Montgomery Township and Somerset County
community since 2017.

Q: Can I drop in for a class without a membership?
A: Yes, drop-ins are welcome. Specific drop-in pricing is available by

Q: I have more questions about pricing or which program is right for me.
