## SECTION 9 — FAQs


**Q:** What is Gracie Farmington Valley?
**A:** Gracie Farmington Valley is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy in Canton,

Connecticut, offering adult Gracie Jiu-Jitsu and Junior Grapplers youth
programs for students from age 4 through adult.


**Q:** Where is Gracie Farmington Valley located?
**A:** 15 Cheryl Drive Suite D, Canton, CT 06019.


**Q:** What is the phone number?
**A:** (860) 500-3829.


**Q:** What is the email address?
**A:** gfvjiujitsu@gmail.com.


**Q:** What programs are offered?
**A:** Gracie Farmington Valley offers adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (Gi and No-Gi)

and Junior Grapplers youth BJJ for ages 4 through 15. Classes run throughout
the week including early morning, midday, and evening sessions.


**Q:** Is there a free trial?
**A:** A free trial class is available for new students.


**Q:** What age can kids start?
**A:** The youngest class, Junior Grapplers, starts at age 4. Classes are

structured by age group: ages 4–5, ages 6–7, and ages 8–15.


**Q:** Are there morning classes for adults?
**A:** Yes. Adult Gi and No-Gi classes run Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at

6:00 AM and 10:00 AM.


**Q:** Are there evening classes for adults?
**A:** Yes. Adult classes run Monday through Thursday evenings as well as Sunday

morning and Sunday open mat.


**Q:** Are there Saturday classes?
**A:** Yes. Junior Grapplers classes run Saturday mornings from 9:00 AM. An adult

Gi class runs Saturday at 10:30 AM.


**Q:** Are there classes on Sunday?
**A:** Yes. Adult No-Gi runs Sunday at 10:00 AM, followed by an Open Mat at

11:00 AM.


**Q:** Are No-Gi classes available?
**A:** Yes. No-Gi classes are available for adults throughout the week including

Monday and Wednesday evenings, Friday mornings, and Sunday mornings.


**Q:** Will my child become a bully from training martial arts?
**A:** Jiu-Jitsu teaches discipline, respect, and self-control. The programs

at Gracie Farmington Valley are designed to build confidence and character,
not aggression.


**Q:** Do parents stay during kids classes?
**A:** Parents are welcome to watch their child's class.


**Q:** Will training help a child with ADHD?
**A:** Martial arts training can support focus, discipline, and structured

physical activity. The academy works with students of different backgrounds
and learning styles.


**Q:** Is jiu-jitsu suitable for non-athletic children?
**A:** Yes. Jiu-Jitsu is accessible to students of all fitness levels. No prior

athletic experience is required.


**Q:** What should I bring to class?
**A:** Confirm with the academy prior to the first class for specific

recommendations on attire and equipment.
