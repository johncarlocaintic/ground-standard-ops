TRIAL / INTRO CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

TRIAL CLASS POLICY:
• The first / intro class is free.
• New students may attend any regularly scheduled class, with two exceptions: Open Mat and Mat Mobility sessions are not eligible for intro or first class visits.
• Advance booking is required at least 24 hours before the intended class time.
• Recommended first-class options for brand-new adult students: Adult Gi at 6:30 PM (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, or Friday) or Saturday at 10:00 AM.

WHAT HAPPENS DURING THE INTRO CLASS (60 MINUTES):
• Welcome and quick check-in (2–3 min): Goals, injuries, and quick questions are confirmed.
• Warm-up (8–12 min): Light movement and basic body mechanics.
• Technique block (15–20 min): 1–2 core fundamentals with clear steps and individual coaching.
• Guided drilling (10–15 min): Practice with a partner while coaches walk the room and assist.
• Optional live rounds (10–15 min): Students may sit out and watch, do light positional sparring under coach supervision, or roll if comfortable and the coach gives the green light. No one is put in the deep end.
• Wrap-up (2–5 min): Questions answered, schedule and membership options discussed.

ARRIVAL TIME:
• First visit: arrive 15–20 minutes early.
• Subsequent visits: 5–10 minutes early is sufficient.
• Early arrival on the first visit accommodates: intro and waiver completion, a facility tour, a review of hygiene and safety rules, and proper partner pairing.

WHAT TO WEAR — FIRST CLASS:

For a Gi Jiu-Jitsu class:
• Best option: Gi (kimono) and belt if available.
• No gi yet: a t-shirt or rashguard and athletic shorts with no pockets or zippers (or leggings/spats) are acceptable. A gi is not required to attend the first class.

For a No-Gi or Wrestling class:
• Rashguard or fitted athletic shirt.
• Shorts with no pockets or zippers, or spats/leggings.
• Wrestling shoes are optional for wrestling classes.

Items not permitted on the mat:
• Jeans, cargo shorts, shorts with zippers, or anything with exposed metal.
• Jewelry of any kind (rings, bracelets, necklaces, watches) must be removed before stepping on the mat.

WHAT TO BRING:
Required for first class:
• Water bottle (highly recommended).
• Small towel if you tend to sweat heavily.
• Flip flops or slides for walking off the mat (helps keep the mats clean; required when using the restroom).

Optional and helpful:
• Mouthguard (recommended for students who plan to spar regularly).
• Knee pads (useful especially for wrestling).

No equipment purchase is required before trying a class.

================================================================================
HYGIENE & SAFETY RULES
================================================================================

Before class:
• Trim fingernails and toenails.
• Shower and use deodorant before training.
• Wear clean training clothes — a fresh shirt or gi is expected every session.

On the mat:
• No shoes on the mat.
• Footwear must be worn when walking off the mat. The restroom requires footwear at all times.
• Notify the coach about any injuries before class begins.
• Tap early and tap often — safety is the priority.
• Students who feel dizzy, nauseous, or injured stop immediately and inform a coach.

MEMBERSHIP & PRICING
================================================================================

PRICING APPROACH:
Membership pricing varies based on several factors, including the number of classes per week a student wants to attend and their specific training goals. Instructors discuss all pricing options and membership plans with students during or right after the free trial class. They assess what program works best for each individual and provide accurate pricing for that student's situation. The trial class is completely free with no commitment required.

All membership and pricing conversations are handled in person by instructors and staff after the intro class or during the trial week. This is the appropriate context for those discussions, as the right plan depends on the individual's goals, schedule, and preferred class types.

MEMBERSHIP STRUCTURE (GENERAL):
Multiple membership plan options are available, ranging from month-to-month to annual agreements. Longer-term commitments offer lower monthly rates. A registration fee applies at sign-up, but it is waived for students who sign up within their 1-week trial period, making the trial period the most financially favorable time to commit.

A limited Founders membership is also available at a reduced monthly rate on a 12-month agreement, with no registration fee. It is offered on a first-come basis and limited to a set number of spots.

Family membership add-ons are available for immediate family members joining on the same plan.

All memberships include unlimited class attendance.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Do I need prior martial arts experience to try a class?
A: No experience is needed. The intro class is designed to be safe, structured, and beginner-friendly — even for people who have never trained before.

Q: Do I need to be in shape before I start?
A: No. Training is how fitness is developed. Coaches scale pace and intensity individually, and students can take breaks at any time.

Q: Do I need to buy a gi or any equipment before my first class?
A: No. No equipment purchase is required before trying a class. Students attending a Gi class without a gi can wear a rashguard or t-shirt and athletic shorts with no pockets or zippers.

Q: What should I wear to my first class?
A: For Gi class: a gi if available, or a rashguard/t-shirt and athletic shorts with no pockets or zippers. For No-Gi or Wrestling: a rashguard or fitted athletic shirt and shorts/spats. No jewelry. Bring flip flops or slides for walking off the mat.

Q: How early do I need to arrive for my first class?
A: Arrive 15–20 minutes early for the first visit to complete the waiver, get a brief tour, and get paired with the right training partner.

Q: Can I book a first class for the same day?
A: Intro class bookings require at least 24 hours advance notice.

Q: Can I attend Open Mat or Mat Mobility as my first class?
A: No. Open Mat and Mat Mobility sessions are not eligible for intro or first class visits. Recommended first-class options for adult beginners are Adult Gi at 6:30 PM (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, or Friday) or Saturday at 10:00 AM.

Q: What classes are available for children?
A: Kids Jiu-Jitsu (ages 4–12) is offered on Monday at 5:30 PM, Wednesday at 5:30 PM (No-Gi), and Saturday at 9:00 AM.

Q: Is there a class specifically for women?
A: Yes. Women's Only Jiu-Jitsu is held every Thursday at 7:30 PM.

Q: What is the Founders membership?
A: The Founders membership is a limited-availability plan on a 12-month agreement with no registration fee. It is offered on a first-come basis and limited to a set number of spots. Instructors will cover the details, including current availability, during or after the free trial class.

Q: Is the registration fee always required?
A: The registration fee is waived for students who sign up within their 1-week trial period. Instructors will explain all sign-up details during or after the intro class.

Q: Can family members join at a discounted rate?
A: Yes. A family membership add-on is available for immediate family members on the same plan. Instructors will walk through the specifics during or after the trial class.

Q: What is the most flexible membership option?
A: A month-to-month option is available with no long-term commitment. Instructors will review all plan options, including the most flexible one, during or after the free trial class.

Q: What is included with all memberships?
A: All memberships include unlimited class attendance. Full plan details are discussed with instructors during or after the trial class.

Q: How do I book my first class?
A: DM "SUGOI" to the academy's social media, or call/text (361) 336-0859. Booking must be made at least 24 hours in advance.

Q: Can parents watch kids classes?
A: Yes. Parents are welcome to observe, as long as it does not distract the room.

Q: What happens after my intro class?
A: After the intro class, staff will ask about your experience, recommend which classes match your goals, and walk through membership options and next steps.
