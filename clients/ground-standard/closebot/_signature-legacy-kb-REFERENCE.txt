CLASSES & PROGRAMS
================================================================================

ADULTS — BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU (GI AND NO-GI)
Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes for adults are available in both Gi and No-Gi formats. Students learn fundamental techniques, develop grappling skills, and build confidence in a supportive and challenging environment.

WOMEN'S CLASS — BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU
A dedicated women's Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class runs every Friday from 5:30 PM to 6:30 PM, led by Yasmin Santana. The class focuses on self-defense techniques and empowerment in a supportive and inclusive environment.

KIDS — BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU
Children's BJJ classes are divided into three age-based groups:
• Kids 1: Ages 5–7
• Kids 2: Ages 8–10
• Kids 3: Ages 11–14

Children's classes are designed to build confidence and develop discipline in a fun and safe environment. Kids classes run on Monday through Friday afternoons and on Saturday mornings.

NOTE TO REVIEWER: A conflict exists between the program description on page 1 of the source document (which states age groups as 4–6 and 7–12) and the schedule table (which shows Kids 1: 5–7y, Kids 2: 8–10y, Kids 3: 11–14y). The schedule table has been used as the primary reference for this KB. See Appendix A, Item 3.

PRIVATE LESSONS
Private lessons are available for students at all levels. Sessions are customized to the individual's specific goals and needs, under the guidance of an experienced instructor. Scheduling details are discussed with staff.

INTRODUCTORY CLASSES
Introductory classes are available by appointment, Monday through Friday, between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Each introductory session is 45 minutes long and accommodates up to 4 students per class. Students learn basic expectations, fundamental jiu-jitsu techniques, and two self-defense techniques. New students are asked to arrive 10 minutes before class.

ITEM 1 — BUSINESS HOURS vs. CLASS SCHEDULE
Source conflict: The FAQ section of the source document states business hours are "6am to 8pm." However, the weekly class schedule table shows evening sessions running until 8:45 PM on multiple weekday nights (e.g., BJJ Intermediate/Advanced on Monday and Wednesday, BJJ Beginners on Tuesday and Thursday all run 7:45 PM–8:45 PM).

Current KB handling: The FAQ answer reflects the stated 6am–8pm figure, with a supplementary note that evening classes may run until 8:45 PM. Client should confirm the correct stated hours.

ITEM 2 — BEGINNER CLASS SCHEDULE AMBIGUITY
Source conflict: The FAQ answer for "beginner class schedule" in the source document reads: "Monday, Wednesday, and Friday: 7am / Tuesday and Thursday: 6am, 7:45pm / Saturday and Sunday: 11am." However, the weekly schedule table shows BJJ Beginners sessions at a broader range of times throughout the week (e.g., 6:30 PM and 7:45 PM on weekday evenings; 10:30 AM on Saturday and Sunday). The FAQ answer appears to describe the Introductory Class (appointment-based, 7am–7pm Monday–Friday) rather than regular BJJ Beginners sessions.

Current KB handling: The full weekly schedule table is used as the authoritative reference for all class times. The Introductory Class schedule is presented separately. Client should clarify what the FAQ's listed beginner times were intended to describe.

---

ITEM 3 — KIDS PROGRAM AGE GROUPS
Source conflict: The program description section on page 1 of the source document lists two age groups for kids: "4- to 6-year-olds" and "7- to 12-year-olds." The weekly schedule table shows three distinct groups: Kids 1 (ages 5–7), Kids 2 (ages 8–10), and Kids 3 (ages 11–14). These descriptions conflict on both the lower bound (age 4 vs. age 5) and the upper bound (age 12 vs. age 14).

Current KB handling: The schedule table's three-group breakdown (Kids 1: 5–7, Kids 2: 8–10, Kids 3: 11–14) is used as the primary reference, as it is more granular and directly tied to actual class sessions. Client should confirm the correct age boundaries for each group.

---

ITEM 4 — UNIFORM PROVISION SCOPE
Source conflict: The FAQ answer in the source document states: "We provide all the uniforms" with no qualification. However, the introductory class information section at the bottom of the same document specifies: "Uniforms are provided for intro classes" — implying that uniform provision may be limited to the introductory class period rather than ongoing membership.

Current KB handling: The more conservative statement ("uniforms are provided for intro classes") is used for the introductory class section. The broader FAQ answer ("uniforms are provided by the academy") is retained for the general FAQ entry, as it reflects what the client chose to publish. Client should confirm whether ongoing members receive uniforms or only introductory-class students do.
