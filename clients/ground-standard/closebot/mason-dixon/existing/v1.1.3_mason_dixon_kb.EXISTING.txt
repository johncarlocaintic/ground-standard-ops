FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: What martial arts does Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu offer?
A: Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu offers No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Adult Striking
(Muay Thai) for adults, and both Jiu-Jitsu and Striking programs for kids
in two age groups: ages 4–7 and ages 8–13.

Q: Is Gi training available?
A: Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu focuses exclusively on No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu. No traditional
Gi uniform is required or used in Jiu-Jitsu classes.

Q: What are the kids program age groups?
A: Kids classes are split into two age groups. The younger group covers ages 4
to 7 with 30-minute sessions. The older group covers ages 8 to 13 with
1-hour sessions. Both age groups have Jiu-Jitsu and Striking classes
available on different days of the week.

Q: Is there open mat available?
A: Yes. Free rolling and open mat are available after all adult Jiu-Jitsu
classes throughout the week. A dedicated Open Mat session also runs on
Fridays from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM.

Q: How many Jiu-Jitsu classes are available for adults per week?
A: There are six adult Jiu-Jitsu classes per week, including early morning
sessions on Monday and Thursday at 6:00 AM, evening sessions Tuesday
through Thursday, and an Advanced Jiu-Jitsu session on Friday evenings.

Q: How many Striking (Muay Thai) classes are available per week?
A: There are two adult Striking sessions per week: Monday evenings from 7:15
to 8:30 PM and Wednesday evenings beginning at 6:15 PM.

Q: What should I bring to my first class?
A: Bring a water bottle and wear athletic clothing suitable for training.
Plan to arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to complete a participation waiver.

Q: Do new students join regular classes?
A: Yes. New students generally join regular scheduled classes. A quick
introductory session may also be available depending on scheduling.

Q: What is the address?
A: Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu is located at 1495 Lincoln Way East, Chambersburg, PA 17201.

Q: How can I reach the gym?
A: The gym can be reached by phone at (717) 402-8999.

Q: What products does the gym sell?
A: The gym offers t-shirts, rash guards, shorts, gloves, and shin guards
for purchase.

Q: Are there early morning classes?
A: Yes. Adult Jiu-Jitsu is offered at 6:00 AM on Monday and Thursday mornings.

Q: Is Advanced Jiu-Jitsu available?
A: Yes. An Adult Advanced Jiu-Jitsu session runs every Friday from 6:00 PM
to 7:00 PM, followed by Open Mat from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
