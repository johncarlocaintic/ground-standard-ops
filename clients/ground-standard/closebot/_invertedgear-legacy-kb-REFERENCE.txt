CLASS DESCRIPTIONS

Fundamentals Class: An hour-long session focused on basic drills and a constraint-led approach to games. This class is designed to ensure a deep understanding and practical application of BJJ fundamentals in an engaging and dynamic way.

Advanced Class: Building on the foundation, the advanced class also runs for an hour and introduces more complex techniques, following a similar drill and games approach. A key component of this class is the inclusion of sparring sessions, allowing students to apply their skills in real-time scenarios under the guidance of experienced instructors.

SAFETY MEASURES
================================================================================

Mat Culture and Expectations: Safety is a core value. Students are taught from day one that protecting themselves and their training partners is a shared responsibility. Aggressive or reckless behavior is not tolerated.

Instructor Supervision: Classes are always supervised by experienced instructors. Coaches monitor the room, correct risky behavior quickly, and adjust pairings when needed.

Games-Based Approach: Most training is done through controlled positional games, which limit dangerous scrambles and encourage thoughtful movement instead of chaotic brawling.

Partner Matching: Coaches actively match students by size, strength, experience level, and temperament to keep training as safe as possible.

Tap Early, Tap Often: Students are reminded constantly that tapping is part of the learning process. There is no shame in tapping—only respect for your own body and your training partners.

Hygiene and Clean Mats: Mats are disinfected after both morning and evening classes, at least twice a day every day. Students are expected to show up with clean gear, trimmed nails, and good hygiene to prevent skin infections and illnesses.

Gi and No-Gi Safety Rules: In gi classes, grip fighting is kept controlled with no yanking or dangerous pulls. In no-gi classes, submissions like heel hooks are reserved for advanced students and taught under strict supervision.

Kids Classes Supervision: Kids' sessions have extra coaches on the mat with small group attention to ensure play stays safe, constructive, and fun. The student-to-instructor ratio is 6:1 for kids classes.

Adult Class Ratio: The student-to-instructor ratio is 10:1 for adult classes.

Optional Sparring for Beginners: Beginners are never forced to spar if they don't feel ready. Even when they do, sparring can be modified to match comfort and skill level.

Injury Reporting Culture: If someone feels discomfort or pain, they are encouraged to immediately stop and inform an instructor. There is no pressure to "tough it out."

SafeSport Certification: Instructors are SafeSport trained and certified, ensuring they understand and uphold national standards for creating a safe, respectful, and abuse-free training environment, especially for minors.

================================================================================
CLEANLINESS & HYGIENE PROTOCOLS
================================================================================

Mat Cleaning: Mats are disinfected after both morning and evening classes—cleaned at least twice a day, every day.

Clean Uniforms Only: All students must wear clean gis, rashguards, and shorts to every class. Dirty gear is not permitted on the mats.

Personal Hygiene: Students are expected to maintain good hygiene including trimming nails, showering regularly, and not training when sick.

Cuts or Skin Conditions: Any cuts, scrapes, or skin issues must be covered properly. If contagious, students should stay off the mats until healed or cleared by a doctor.

Footwear Policy: No shoes on the mats. Students must wear sandals or flip-flops anytime they step off the mat, especially when going to the restroom.

POLICIES & CULTURE
================================================================================

BULLYING & HARASSMENT POLICY

Zero Tolerance for Bullying: This includes physical intimidation, verbal abuse, teasing, exclusion, or online harassment. Any behavior that intentionally harms or intimidates another person will be addressed immediately.

Respect on and off the mats: All members are expected to treat each other with kindness and respect—whether in class, during open mats, or on social media. This includes respecting personal boundaries, space, and communication styles.

Harassment-Free Zone: Harassment—whether based on race, gender, sexual orientation, age, religion, or any other personal characteristic—has no place in the academy.

Reporting and Confidentiality: If someone experiences or witnesses bullying or harassment, they should report it to an instructor or staff member as soon as possible. All reports will be handled discreetly and respectfully.

Disciplinary Action: Depending on the severity of the incident, disciplinary action may include a warning, suspension, or permanent removal from the academy.

Community Culture: The academy is proud to foster a community where everyone can train, learn, and grow in a positive environment.

RESPECTFUL CONDUCT & SPORTSMANSHIP

Respect at All Levels: Students are expected to show respect to instructors, teammates, visitors, and themselves—on and off the mats.

Supportive Training Environment: The academy fosters a team-first attitude. This means encouraging others, offering help when needed, and celebrating each other's progress.

Tap Early, Release Quickly: Training is hard but safe. Submissions should always be applied with control and released immediately when a partner taps.

Leave Ego at the Door: Everyone is there to learn. Whether rolling with a white belt or a black belt, mutual respect and humility are essential.

ACCOMMODATIONS
================================================================================

SPECIAL NEEDS & DISABILITIES

Accessibility: The academy has a wheelchair-accessible entrance via a ramp. The staff is open to working with students and families to find ways to support individual needs.

ALLERGY ACCOMMODATIONS

Allergy Awareness: Students and parents are asked to inform the academy of any known allergies—especially severe ones like food, insect, or latex allergies—so appropriate precautions can be taken. While not a designated allergen-free facility, the academy does its best to minimize exposure risks (e.g., limiting food in training areas).

Emergency Protocols: If a student carries an EpiPen or other emergency medication, parents or the student (if age-appropriate) are encouraged to let instructors know where it is kept and how to use it. Staff are trained to respond quickly and call emergency services if needed.

Communication: The more the academy knows, the better they can help. The staff is happy to work with families to develop a plan for students with allergies or medical concerns to ensure everyone can train with confidence and peace of mind.

Q: Are there specific classes focused on self-defense?
A: Self-defense is a core part of the curriculum—especially in beginner and fundamentals classes. While there are no separate "self-defense only" classes, many skills taught in regular Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes are directly applicable to self-defense—especially how to stay safe on the ground, escape bad positions, and neutralize larger opponents.
