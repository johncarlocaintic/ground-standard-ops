================================================================================

Full Business Name: Artistry BJJ
Industry: Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu / Mixed Martial Arts
Location: Houston, TX (East Houston)

SECTION 5 — PROGRAM DESCRIPTIONS

================================================================================

ADULT NO-GI BJJ
No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes for adults. Covers technique, positional
understanding, and controlled live training. Available Monday morning, Tuesday
midday, and Monday, Wednesday, and Friday evenings.

ADULT GI BJJ
Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes for adults using the traditional kimono. Available
Wednesday morning, and Tuesday and Thursday midday and evening.

ADULT MMA
Professional, structured Mixed Martial Arts training. Focused on self-defense
applications. Available Monday, Wednesday, and Friday evenings.

KIDS GI BJJ
Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes for children ages 5 to 13 using the traditional gi.
Builds confidence, discipline, and resilience. Available Monday, Wednesday, and
Friday evenings at 5:30 PM, and Saturday morning at 11:30 AM.

KIDS STRIKING
Striking-focused classes for children. Available Tuesday and Thursday evenings
at 5:30 PM.

OPEN MAT
Unstructured training session for drilling and rolling. Saturday mornings only
at 11:30 AM.

NEW STUDENT CLASS EXPERIENCE
New students join regular classes directly. The instructor is made aware of new
students and pairs them with more experienced training partners. Class content
is adjusted toward fundamentals when new students are present. Sparring is
optional.

SECTION 8 — POLICIES

================================================================================

Sign-Up Process:
New students can walk in to any regular class without an appointment. No prior
registration is required to try a class. Formal gym enrollment is completed in
person at the academy.

Membership Pause:
Membership holds require 30 days notice. A doctor's note waives the notice
requirement.

Cancellation:
Membership cancellation requires 30 days notice.

Payment Methods:
Monthly memberships are paid by card. Yearly memberships are paid by cash.

Equipment:
Members are required to have their own gym gear. Gear is available for purchase
at the academy. First-time visitors should wear comfortable clothing with no
pockets or zippers.

Additional Fees:
A sign-up fee applies at enrollment. The sign-up fee includes gear.

Guests:
Guests are welcome at the academy.

Military and First Responder Discount:
Discounts are available for military members and first responders.

Family Discount:
A buy-two-get-two discount is available for families within the same household.

Photography and Video:
Photos and videos are permitted at the academy. Techniques shown during class
are not to be posted online.

Conduct Policy:
Anyone who acts out of line or threatens the safety of others will be removed
from the academy.

Parents:
Parents are welcome to watch their children's classes.

Special Needs and Disabilities:
Students with special needs or disabilities are paired with more experienced
training partners to ensure a safe and productive experience.

SECTION 9 — FAQs

================================================================================

Q: What programs does Artistry BJJ offer?
A: Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and MMA for both adults and kids. Adult programs include
No-Gi BJJ, Gi BJJ, and MMA. Kids programs include Gi BJJ (ages 5-13) and
Striking. Saturday Open Mat is also available.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: A one-week free trial is available with no commitment required.

Q: Do I need experience to start?
A: No experience is required. New students join regular classes and are paired
with experienced training partners. Class content is adjusted to be fundamentals-
focused when new students are present. Sparring is optional.

Q: What should I wear to my first class?
A: Comfortable clothing with no pockets or zippers is appropriate for a first
visit. Gym gear is available for purchase at the academy for enrolled members.

Q: Are there morning classes?
A: Morning classes are available. No-Gi BJJ runs Monday mornings at 5:00 AM and
Gi BJJ runs Wednesday mornings at 5:00 AM. Midday classes run Tuesday and
Thursday at 12:00 PM.

Q: Are there classes for kids?
A: Kids Gi BJJ runs Monday, Wednesday, and Friday evenings at 5:30 PM and
Saturday mornings at 11:30 AM. Kids Striking runs Tuesday and Thursday evenings
at 5:30 PM. The kids program is for children ages 5 to 13.

Q: Is there a women's-only class?
A: A dedicated women's-only class is not currently offered.

Q: Are there family discounts?
A: A buy-two-get-two discount is available for families within the same household.

Q: Is there a drop-in option?
A: Membership pricing and drop-in details are reviewed with students based on
training goals and schedule preferences. A one-week free trial is available with
no commitment required.

Q: Can I watch a class before deciding to join?
A: Prospective students are welcome to observe a class before committing.

Q: Who is the head instructor?
A: Felipe Rocha is the owner and head instructor. He has nearly two decades of
experience in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu as both a competitor and coach.

Q: Are discounts available for military or first responders?
A: Discounts are available for military members and first responders.

Q: What is the cancellation or pause policy?
A: Membership cancellation and pause requests both require 30 days notice.
A doctor's note waives the notice requirement for a hold.

Q: Can parents watch their kids' classes?
A: Parents are welcome to watch their children's classes.

Q: What forms of payment are accepted?
A: Monthly memberships are paid by card. Yearly memberships are paid by cash.

Q: Is there an Open Mat session?
A: Open Mat is available on Saturdays at 11:30 AM.

Q: Does Artistry BJJ have a second location?
A: A second location is listed at 181 Market St, Suite 106, Georgetown, TX.
