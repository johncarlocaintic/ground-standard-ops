## SECTION 4 — PROGRAM DESCRIPTIONS


GRACIE COMBATIVES
The beginner-level Gracie Jiu-Jitsu program covering 36 essential techniques
for real-life self-defense. No sparring and no prior experience required.
New students are welcome to join any Gracie Combatives class directly. The
instructor teaches all Gracie Combatives classes treating every student as if
they have no prior experience.

REFLEX DEVELOPMENT
Intermediate-level program for students progressing through the Gracie
curriculum. Available Wednesday, Thursday, and Saturday.

MASTER CYCLE
Advanced program for students who have completed Gracie Combatives. Features
ground techniques, transitions, submissions, and live rolling. Available
Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday.

GRACIE BULLYPROOF (YOUTH — AGES 5–12)

## SECTION 5 — TRIAL / FIRST CLASS INFORMATION


A 10-calendar-day free trial is available for new students. Drop-in attendance
is also available.

Sign-up process:
1. Sign the digital enrollment form.
2. Sign the participation waiver.
3. Come in for the trial class.
4. After the trial, new students may convert to a membership.

New trial students receive a loaner uniform at no charge. A credit card is
required as a security deposit for the loaner uniform.

No equipment is needed for the first class.

Guests may observe a class before deciding to join.

A tour of the facility is available upon request.


## SECTION 6 — MEMBERSHIP AND PRICING


Membership pricing is not published. Pricing varies based on the days attended
per week. Membership options and accurate pricing are discussed with new
students following the 10-day free trial.

A processing fee applies at the start of membership. The uniform is included
in the processing fee.

Membership Pause: Members may pause their account for up to 2 months per year.

Cancellation: No cancellation fee. A 60-day notice period is required. Members
may continue training during the 60-day notice period.

Payment: Credit or debit cards only.

## SECTION 8 — POLICIES


Uniform:
A white gi is required. Loaner uniforms are available for trial students.
Members may bring their own gi from outside the academy, provided it is white.
The uniform is included in the processing fee upon becoming a member.

Sparring:
Beginners are not permitted to spar until they have earned 3 stripes on their
belt or completed 60 classes.

Belt Promotions:
- Gracie Combatives adults: 80 classes and 8 months in the program required.
- Youth students: based on attendance.
- Kids attendance is tracked via attendance cards.

Hygiene:
No bare feet in the bathroom. All uniforms must be white so cleanliness can
be verified.

Facilities:
Three dressing rooms available. No dedicated locker rooms. Wi-Fi available.
Parking available in the surface lot in front of the building.

Parents:
Parents may watch their children's classes.

Women's Classes:
Dedicated women's-only classes are no longer offered. A women's community
text group is active among current female members.

Guests:
Guests may attend a class or observe.

Photography:
No photography restrictions.

Events:
Birthday parties and private events are available.

Online Resources:
Students receive access to Gracie University's video curriculum.

Discounts:
- Military, first responders, and students: discounts available.
- Family: 20% off for the second family member; 30% off for each additional
  family member.

Special Needs / Allergies:
No accommodation program is currently in place.

Bullying / Harassment Policy:
No formal policy is currently in place.

Community Events:
None currently offered.
