================================================================================
GYM STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Hammer Sports & Performance serves two types of training members: competitive fighters (fight team) and hobbyist practitioners. All membership types include gym access.

Average class sizes range from approximately 5 students in morning sessions to 15 students in evening sessions, with an average of 8 to 12 students per class.

No live sparring is conducted during introductory sessions. Intro attendees participate in technique and pad work only.

================================================================================
MEMBERSHIP OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Hammer Sports & Performance offers multiple membership tiers to accommodate different training goals and schedules — from children's programs to conditioning-only memberships to unlimited and private-session packages. Membership options cover Kids (ages 5–12), Teen (ages 13 through high school), Conditioning, Unlimited, and Ultimate tiers with private session add-ons.

All memberships include gym access. Membership commitments are available across different term lengths. Specific pricing, commitment lengths, and the membership option that best fits a prospect's goals and schedule are discussed directly with staff.

Factors that affect which membership is the right fit include age, training goals, preferred disciplines, desired class frequency, and whether private sessions are part of the training plan. Staff assess these factors to help new members select the appropriate tier.

Membership pricing varies based on several factors like the number of classes per week you'd want to attend and your specific training goals. Instructors will discuss all the pricing options and membership plans with you during or right after your free trial class. They'll be able to assess what program works best for you and provide accurate pricing for your situation. The trial class is completely free with no commitment required.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: How much does membership cost?
A: Membership pricing varies based on several factors like the number of classes per week you'd want to attend and your specific training goals. Instructors will discuss all the pricing options and membership plans with you during or right after your free trial class. They'll be able to assess what program works best for you and provide accurate pricing for your situation. The trial class is completely free with no commitment required.

Q: What programs do you offer?
A: Hammer Sports & Performance offers Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (Gi and No-Gi), Wrestling, Kickboxing, Muay Thai, MMA, and Kettlebell conditioning. There is a dedicated Little Hammer program for kids ages 5 to 12, and a Girls Only Wrestling class starting March 22, 2026.

Q: Do you have classes for kids?
A: Yes. The Little Hammer program serves children ages 5 to 12, with classes in BJJ, Wrestling, MMA, and Kickboxing running Monday through Friday from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM, plus new Saturday classes beginning March 21, 2026.

Q: Is there a girls-only class?
A: Yes. A Girls Only Wrestling class launches Sunday, March 22, 2026 at 10:00 AM and runs weekly.

Q: How large are the classes?
A: Morning classes typically have around 5 students. Evening classes average around 15 students. Across all sessions, the typical class size ranges from 8 to 12 students.

Q: What is the difference between Gi and No-Gi BJJ?
A: Gi BJJ is trained wearing a traditional uniform (the Gi), which is used in grips and techniques. No-Gi BJJ is practiced in shorts and a rash guard, without the traditional uniform, and relies more on body control and clinch work.

Q: What happens at an introductory class?
A: Intro sessions are technique and pad work focused. There is no live sparring during introductory visits. New students attend alongside regular class participants.

Q: Can I watch a class before signing up?
A: Visitors are welcome to come in and observe classes to get a feel for the gym before committing.

Q: Does Muay Thai have its own classes?
A: Yes. Muay Thai classes are on the schedule — Wednesday at 7:30 PM and Saturday at 9:00 AM (starting March 21, 2026). On the GSPro booking platform, Muay Thai is listed under the Kickboxing category when booking.

Q: What is Open Mat?
A: Open Mat sessions are unstructured training time where members can drill techniques, roll, or work on specific skills. Open Mat is available Wednesday at 10:00 AM and Friday at 7:30 PM.

Q: Do all memberships include gym access?
A: Yes. All membership tiers include gym access.

Q: Where can I find the live schedule?
A: The live class schedule is available through the gym's website at www.hammertrained.com.

Q: How do I sign up?
A: New members sign up in person at the gym. Staff guide new members through the enrollment process and help identify the right membership tier based on their goals.

Q: How do I get to the gym?
A: The gym is located at 1719 Union Ave, Hazlet, NJ 07730, past the Chinese restaurant on the corner. From Route 35, head toward Hazlet Township and make your way to Union Avenue (County Road 36). Nearby landmarks include Veterans Memorial Park and Hazlet Plaza.

SECTION A2: INCONSISTENCIES FLAGGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FLAG 1 — Kids Pricing: Identical Annual Rate Across Age Groups
  Source: Pricing section of client PDF
  Issue: Both the 5–7 and 8–12 age brackets show the same 12-month rate of $89/month,
  despite having different 6-month rates ($75 vs $99). This may be intentional
  (flat annual rate regardless of age group) or a data entry error.
  Status: Pending client confirmation. Bot-facing content defers all pricing to staff.

FLAG 2 — Teen Membership Qualifier: "or Blue Belt"
  Source: Pricing section of client PDF
  Issue: The teen membership is defined as ages 13 through high school "or blue belt."
  The blue belt qualifier implies an adult practitioner at a rank milestone could be
  categorized under the teen tier, which is non-standard. May affect how staff qualify
  prospects.
  Status: Pending client clarification. Bot-facing content does not reference this qualifier.

FLAG 3 — Monday 8:00 AM Class Labeled Incorrectly in Internal Note
  Source: Internal staff note within client PDF ("Monday 8am as an option for standup
  — that's the No-Gi time, 9am is the standup")
  Issue: The 8:00 AM Monday slot is No-Gi Fundamentals (grappling), not a standup/
  striking class. The 9:00 AM Monday slot is Kickboxing (standup). This distinction
  is important for routing prospects to the correct class type.
  Status: Accounted for in schedule section. No action needed unless client wants
  additional clarification language added.

FLAG 4 — Intro / Trial Class Policy: "Free Trial" Not Explicitly Confirmed
  Source: Client PDF — no explicit mention of a free introductory class offer
  Issue: The source material does not state whether the first class or introductory
  visit is free of charge. This is a significant bot-facing gap, as prospects commonly
  ask about trying before committing.
  Status: FLAGGED — Pending client confirmation. Current bot-facing KB directs
  prospects to staff and website for enrollment details. Do not add "free trial"
  language until confirmed.

FLAG 5 — No Phone Number or Email Address Provided
  Source: Client PDF
  Issue: The PDF does not include a phone number or contact email for the gym.
  Only the website (www.hammertrained.com) and physical address are available.
  Status: Flagged as data gap. Bot-facing FAQ routes all direct contact to the website.
  Recommend client supply phone and email for v1.1.0.

FLAG 6 — New Saturday/Sunday Classes: Launch Date Not Yet Passed at Build Time
  Source: Schedule image in client PDF
  Issue: Saturday Kids Class and Girls Only Sundays are noted as starting 3/21 and
  3/22/2026, which is 4 days from the build date (March 17, 2026). These classes
  are included in the schedule section but should be confirmed as launched and
  active before the bot promotes them as currently available.
  Status: Included with launch date noted. Recommend verifying and updating language
  to remove the date qualifier after 3/22/2026.
