REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR TRIAL CLASS BOOKING
================================================================================

To book a trial class, the following information is collected for safety, legal compliance, and communication purposes:

REQUIRED FIELDS:
1. Full Name (First and Last)
2. Email Address
3. Phone Number
4. Date of Birth
5. Parent/Guardian Information (if student is under 18)

WHY THIS INFORMATION IS COLLECTED:

FULL NAME (FIRST AND LAST):
Full legal name is required to complete the student profile and register for the free class. The profile system requires first and last name to be entered.

Additionally, full legal name is needed for liability insurance requirements, personalized welcome experience with instructor, and legal waiver requirements (state regulations require full legal names on participation waivers signed on first day).

EMAIL ADDRESS:
Email address is required to complete the student profile and register for the free class. Email is used to send class confirmations with important details (what to wear, when to arrive, what to expect) and notifications about schedule changes or cancellations.

Information is kept private and secure, never shared with third parties.

PHONE NUMBER:
Phone number is required to complete the student profile and register for the free class. Phone is used for text reminders about classes, emergency contact if running late or unable to find the location, and last-minute communication for schedule changes or cancellations.

Liability insurance requires emergency contact information for all participants.

DATE OF BIRTH:
Date of birth is required to complete the student profile and register for the free class. The profile system requires the actual date of birth (not age) to be entered in the system.

Date of birth confirms age-appropriate program placement across the seven class options (Kids 4-5 Kickboxing, Kids 6-8 BJJ, Kids 6-11 Kickboxing, Kids 9-13 BJJ, Kids 12-15 Kickboxing, Adult BJJ, Adult Kickboxing), ensures safety considerations, and meets insurance requirements.

PARENT/GUARDIAN INFORMATION (FOR MINORS UNDER 18):
Parent or guardian information (including their date of birth) is required because the child's profile is attached to the parent/guardian profile in the system. The parent/guardian profile must be completed to register the child for the free class.

Additionally, state law requires minors cannot sign legal waivers (parent/guardian must consent), emergency medical decisions require immediate parent/guardian contact, and financial responsibility requires parent/guardian authorization for payments.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: What is the minimum age for kids classes?
A: Age 4. Kids 4-5 Kickboxing is the youngest class. There is no BJJ option for ages 4-5.

Q: Do you offer wrestling or MMA?
A: Ballantyne Martial Arts offers Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Kickboxing. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu covers grappling and ground fighting, which is the closest discipline to wrestling.

Q: Can I watch a class before deciding to join?
A: Yes. Visitors are welcome to observe classes during regular hours.

Q: What should I wear to my first martial arts class?
A: Athletic clothing works well - shorts and a t-shirt, or athletic pants (sweatpants or yoga pants). Pockets are discouraged for safety. Shoes and socks must be removed before training on the mats.

Q: Do I need to bring any equipment for my first class?
A: No equipment is needed for trial classes.

Q: What types of martial arts classes do you offer?
A: Kickboxing and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

Q: What is the schedule for beginner classes?
A: All regular classes accommodate beginners. New students may join any scheduled class based on their availability.

Q: Can parents watch their children during classes?
A: Yes. Parents are welcome to watch. Parental involvement is kept to a minimum unless there are extenuating circumstances.

Q: Do you offer family discounts?
A: Yes. Families are encouraged to train together and receive discounted rates. Specific pricing information discussed with staff.

Q: Are there classes specifically for women?
A: Women train in all regular classes. Women's Self Defense seminars are offered approximately once per quarter.

Q: Do you have locker room facilities?
A: Restrooms are available for changing. No locker rooms.

Q: Is there parking available at the facility?
A: Yes. The facility is located in a strip mall with ample parking.

Q: Can I pause my membership for a vacation or other reasons?
A: Yes. Month-to-month memberships can be paused for extended vacations or illness.

Q: How is progress and attendance monitored for children's classes?
A: All students sign in on the computer when they arrive. Progress is monitored by instructors during classes.

Q: Can I get a tour of the gym before deciding to join?
A: Yes. Tours are available during class hours.

Q: Can I bring a guest to the gym?
A: Yes. Guests need to sign the waiver when they arrive.

Q: Do you offer discounts for military, first responders, or students?
A: Yes. Military and First Responders receive discounts on memberships. Summer College Student memberships are also available. Specific pricing details discussed with staff.

Q: Can I host a birthday party or private event at the gym?
A: Yes. Details must be arranged with staff.

Q: Do you have Wi-Fi available in the gym?
A: Yes. Wi-Fi is available for guests.

Q: Are there any specific rules regarding photography or filming in the gym?
A: Photography is permitted. Photographers should remain in the lobby area unless attending a special event where the mat area is more open than usual.

Q: Are there any online classes or resources available?
A: Online resources are currently in development.

SAFETY & TRAINING
================================================================================

SAFETY MEASURES:
• Multiple instructors and black belts present during all classes
• Effort levels specified during training to ensure proper technique learning
• Industry-standard mats over 1.5 inches thick cushion all falls
• Gear available for harder sparring and drilling
• Mouth guards required for any sparring or non-drill BJJ rolling
• Progressive gear requirements as students advance in rank

TRAINING PHILOSOPHY:
• Students train as individuals but support each other as a team
• Classes incorporate both sport and street self-defense perspectives
• Most classes include common street attack scenarios and situations
• Focus on continuous improvement and becoming a better version of oneself

STUDENT-TO-INSTRUCTOR RATIO:
• Approximately 1 instructor per 7 students
• Additional instructors and black belts always near the mat

CLEANLINESS & HYGIENE:

Personal Hygiene:
Students maintain clean bodies, clothes, and gear.

Facility Cleaning:
• Mats swept and mopped daily
• Restrooms cleaned daily
• Lobby area cleaned daily
