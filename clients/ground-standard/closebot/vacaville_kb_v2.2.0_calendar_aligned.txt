SECTION 1 — BUSINESS INFORMATION
================================================================================ Full Business Name: Vacaville Grappling Academy

There is no current program for children under 7. What We Do Not Currently Offer (explicit facts for lead questions):

- No Sunday classes. The academy does not run classes on Sundays.
- No weekend classes of any kind. All classes run Monday through Friday only.

- No standalone wrestling program. Wrestling techniques appear in the No-Gi curriculum.

The academy does not offer them. Training Philosophy: The academy culture prioritizes respecting others at all times
and emphasizes learning skills over winning. The goal is fun, respect, and continuous
improvement — not competition results. Coach Nick's Protective Security training and
career experience are woven into every practice to develop practical self-defense

a framework that uses targeted restrictions to shape training scenarios, allowing
athletes to discover practical solutions organically rather than through repetitive

A: Yes. Vacaville Grappling Academy uses the Constraint-Led Approach (CLA) as part
of its training methodology. Q: What is the Constraint-Led Approach (CLA)?
A: The Constraint-Led Approach (CLA) is a skill development framework rooted in
ecological dynamics research. Rather than relying on repetitive drilling of isolated
techniques, CLA uses targeted restrictions — called constraints — to shape training
scenarios. Athletes are guided to discover practical solutions organically through
dynamic, interactive situations. The three types of constraints used are task
constraints (the goal or objective of the drill), environmental constraints (factors
related to space, time, or conditions), and individual constraints (limitations
related to the athlete's capabilities or focus). Training under CLA develops
adaptability, pattern recognition under pressure, and skills that transfer directly
to live sparring. Q: Do you offer online classes?
