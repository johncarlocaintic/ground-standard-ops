SECTION 6: TRIAL CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

Trial Offer: One-week free trial is available to all new students.

During the trial, students may attend any class listed on the regular schedule.
There are no separate introductory-only classes.

Trial Class Arrival Instructions:
- Arrive 20 minutes before the class start time.
- Use the time before class to change into a uniform (loaners are provided),
  sign the participation waiver, and receive a brief orientation.

Uniforms: Loaner uniforms are provided for the duration of the trial week.
Students are not required to bring their own gear for the trial.

Age Restrictions for Trial Participation:
- Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu: Available for students ages 12 and older only.
- Judo: Available for students ages 5 and older.
- Karate: Available for students ages 5 and older.
- Champion Conditioning: Open to all students.

What to Wear Under the Uniform:
Athletic or yoga-style clothing is appropriate to wear underneath the loaner
uniform during trial classes.

APPENDIX: INCONSISTENCIES IDENTIFIED IN SOURCE DOCUMENT
================================================================================

The following issues were identified during source document analysis. These
are flagged for client review and correction before the next KB version update.

INCONSISTENCY 1 — SUNDAY ROW IN SCHEDULE TABLE
The PDF schedule table displays a "Sunday" label in the leftmost column as a
visual row-group header. However, no classes appear under a Sunday column in
the table, and the listed business hours explicitly state the academy is closed
on Sunday. This is assessed as a PDF layout rendering artifact. The KB reflects
Sunday as a closed day with no classes, consistent with the stated business
hours. Client should confirm whether Sunday classes exist or whether the label
should be removed from any public-facing schedule materials.

INCONSISTENCY 2 — FACILITY ADDRESS PRESENTATION
The document header clearly states the facility address as "4016 Strawberry Rd,
Pasadena, TX 77504." A separate section within the document body states
"Facility Address: [See website for latest]," which appears to be an
unresolved placeholder. The KB uses the header address as authoritative.
Client should confirm whether this address is current or if a website redirect
is preferred for leads.

INCONSISTENCY 3 — INFORMAL OWNER NOTE IN FORMAL DOCUMENT
The staff section of the PDF contains an informal, first-person owner note
about how the academy handles students who live far away. This note was not
formatted as structured business information. It has been sanitized and
converted into a factual policy statement in the KB (see Section 10: Faraway
Students). Client should review whether this policy is accurate as written
and whether it should be included in the chatbot's accessible information.

INCONSISTENCY 4 — MMA-FOCUSED BOXING LISTED AS PROGRAM
The source document lists "MMA-focused Boxing (as progression)" among the
programs offered. However, this discipline does not appear in the class
schedule as a standalone class, and it is explicitly described as a progression
track rather than a direct enrollment program. Per client instruction, the
four confirmed enrollable programs are: Adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Adult
Karate, Adult Judo, and Adult Strength & Conditioning. MMA Boxing has been
excluded from the KB's program listings. Client should confirm whether MMA
Boxing is currently active as a standalone class or remains only a progression
track, and update the source document accordingly.
