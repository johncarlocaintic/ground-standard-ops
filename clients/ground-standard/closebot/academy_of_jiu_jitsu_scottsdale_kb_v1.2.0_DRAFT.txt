SECTION 1 — BUSINESS INFORMATION
================================================================================

Full Business Name: Academy of Jiu-Jitsu Scottsdale
Industry: Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)
Location: Scottsdale, AZ

Description: Academy of Jiu-Jitsu Scottsdale is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu school
located in The Pavilions at Talking Stick Shopping Center in Scottsdale, Arizona.
The Academy offers adult BJJ programs across beginner, intermediate, and advanced
levels, as well as Kids BJJ programs split into two age groups. All instructors
hold recognized martial arts certifications and have years of teaching experience.
Classes are designed for beginners through advanced students, with instruction
customized to individual skill levels and goals.

Programs Offered:
- Little Tigers BJJ (Ages 5–7)
- Tigers BJJ (Ages 8–13)
- Adult BJJ Fundamentals (Beginner to Intermediate — White Belt to Blue Belt path)
- Fundamentals Live Training (Saturday only — intermediate bridging class)
- Adult BJJ Advanced (Intermediate to Advanced curriculum)

What Is Not on the Schedule:
- No Sunday classes. The published schedule covers Monday through Saturday only.
- No Wednesday evening adult classes. Wednesday adult training is morning only.
- No Friday evening adult classes. Friday adult training is morning only.

techniques and sequences in real-time, resistance-based scenarios that replicate

SECTION 8 — FAQs
================================================================================

Q: What programs are available for kids?
A: Two kids programs are offered — Little Tigers for ages 5 to 7, and Tigers for
ages 8 to 13. Little Tigers runs Monday through Thursday afternoons and Saturday
mornings. Tigers runs Tuesday through Thursday afternoons and Saturday mornings.

Q: What is Adult BJJ Fundamentals?
A: Adult Fundamentals covers beginner and intermediate BJJ, including self-defense,
BJJ positions, techniques, and concepts. It is structured as the path from
White Belt to Blue Belt.

Q: What is Fundamentals Live Training?
A: Fundamentals Live Training is a Saturday-only class that bridges the gap between
drilling techniques and full-intensity sparring. Students practice techniques in
real-time, resistance-based scenarios that replicate a live roll.

Q: What is Adult BJJ Advanced?
A: Adult Advanced covers a wide range of intermediate and advanced BJJ curriculum.
It runs Monday, Tuesday, Thursday evenings and Saturday mornings.

Q: Is there a free trial class?
A: A free trial class is available with no commitment required. Booking is available
online at academyofjiujitsuscottsdale.com or by calling (480) 270-6040.

Q: Do I need experience to start?
A: No experience is required for the Fundamentals or Kids programs. Classes are
designed for all levels from complete beginners through advanced students, with
instruction customized to individual skill levels and goals.

Q: Are there morning classes available?
A: Adult morning classes run Monday, Wednesday, and Friday from 11:00am to 12:00pm
(Fundamentals) and Monday and Friday from 12:00pm to 1:00pm (Advanced).

Q: Do you offer classes on weekends?
A: Saturday classes are available for all programs — Little Tigers, Tigers, Adult
Fundamentals, Fundamentals Live Training, and Adult Advanced. No Sunday classes
are currently scheduled.

Q: Where is the Academy located?
A: The Academy is at 8969 E. Talking Stick Way, Suite C-1, Scottsdale, AZ 85250,
inside The Pavilions at Talking Stick Shopping Center.
