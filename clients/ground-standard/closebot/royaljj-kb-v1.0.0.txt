================================================================
GYM IDENTITY
================================================================

Royal Jiu-Jitsu Academy Queens is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy in the Kew Gardens neighborhood of Queens, New York. The academy offers Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu for adults and youth in a beginner-friendly training environment.

Location: 82-77 116th Street, Queens, NY 11418 (Kew Gardens)
Phone: (516) 789-3131
Email: royaljiujitsuqueens@gmail.com
Website: royaljiujitsuqueens.com

Head Instructor: Matheus Lutes — black belt under Marcelo Garcia; internationally recognized competitor.

================================================================
WHO TRAINS HERE
================================================================

Adults (ages 18+) and youth (ages 5-13). The academy welcomes beginners and trains members of all backgrounds and fitness levels. Programs emphasize technical instruction, character-building, and mental resilience alongside physical training.

================================================================
PROGRAMS
================================================================

Bookable for trial:
- Adult Fundamentals BJJ — ages 18 and over, 60 minutes per session. Designed for new students through experienced practitioners. This is the standard entry point for any adult new to the academy.
- Kids BJJ — ages 5 through 13, 60 minutes per session. This is the standard entry point for any youth new to the academy.

Not currently offered for new-student trial booking (members or invite-only programming):
- Adult Advanced BJJ
- Adult All Levels BJJ

================================================================
TRAINING ENVIRONMENT
================================================================

The academy maintains padded floors and wall mats with daily sanitization. Training emphasizes a welcoming environment for beginners while offering technical depth for competitors.

================================================================
TRIAL CLASS
================================================================

The Adult Fundamentals BJJ and Kids BJJ sessions are the entry point for new students. New leads start with one of those sessions depending on age band.

================================================================
AGE BAND ROUTING
================================================================

- Ages 5-13: Kids BJJ
- Ages 14-17: No dedicated calendar at this band. A coach from the academy will follow up directly to discuss options.
- Ages 18 and over: Adult Fundamentals BJJ
- Under 5: No dedicated calendar. A coach will follow up directly.
