PROGRAMS & CLASSES
================================================================================

CHILDREN'S MARTIAL ARTS (Ages 4-12)

45-minute classes teaching a comprehensive martial arts curriculum at an
age-appropriate intensity level. Classes are held Monday through Friday from
5:00 PM to 6:45 PM (two time slots available) and on Saturday mornings from
11:00 AM to 12:45 PM (two time slots available). All children's classes
accommodate beginners. Students who perform exceptionally well in children's
classes may be assessed by the instructor for placement in adult classes
regardless of their age.

ADULT MARTIAL ARTS (Ages 13+)

45-minute classes teaching the same martial arts curriculum as the children's
program but at a significantly higher intensity level. Adult classes are
available Monday through Friday at 7:00 PM and on Saturday at 2:00 PM. There
is no upper age limit. Adults starting at age 13 join this program. New
students are welcome to join the regular scheduled classes.

HEALTH & FITNESS (Adults Only)

Exercise-based classes designed for adults who want a challenging fitness
experience without focusing on martial arts techniques. Classes include
cardio, strength training, yoga, H.I.I.T training, and additional fitness
modalities. Health & Fitness classes run Monday, Thursday, and Friday from
8:00 PM to 8:45 PM.

WEAPONS CLASS (Children & Adults Combined)

A combined class open to both children and adults covering weapons training.
Held on Tuesday from 4:00 PM to 4:45 PM and Saturday from 1:00 PM to 1:15 PM.

SPARRING CLASS (Children & Adults Combined)

A combined class open to both children and adults focusing on sparring
techniques and application. Held on Thursday from 4:00 PM to 4:45 PM and
Saturday from 1:15 PM to 2:00 PM.

CURRICULUM NOTE

The children's and adult martial arts programs teach the same curriculum. The
only difference between the two is the intensity level. Adult classes are
considerably more intense. High-performing children's students may be moved to
adult classes at the instructor's discretion, regardless of age.

================================================================================
AFTERSCHOOL PROGRAM
================================================================================

PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The Afterschool Program is a structured after-school service that prioritizes
academic homework assistance alongside martial arts training. It is designed
specifically to support working parents by providing comprehensive after-school
coverage, extended hours, and school closure day coverage.

DAILY PROGRAM STRUCTURE

Homework assistance is the first priority. Staff ensure homework is completed
correctly and guide students through understanding what they are learning, not
just completing assignments. Following homework time, the program includes:

- Martial arts training (45 minutes, 3 times per week, included in program)
- Academic reinforcement including reading, writing, penmanship, and math
  (based on individual student needs)
- Structured social activities
- Group games and team-building activities
- Free time to relax and enjoy a snack

EXTENDED HOURS & COVERAGE

- Late pickup available until 8:00 PM, Monday through Friday
- Half-day pickups available at no additional charge
- Open at 7:00 AM on days when school is closed, providing a full day of
  structured activities to support working parents

TAX BENEFITS

The Afterschool Program is tax-deductible. The academy provides tax
documentation for credit and deduction purposes.

ENROLLMENT

Space in the Afterschool Program is very limited and enrollment fills quickly.
Prospective families are encouraged to inquire about availability promptly.

================================================================================
MARTIAL ARTS SUMMER PROGRAM
================================================================================

PROGRAM DATES

The Martial Arts Summer Program begins on the first business day after school
ends and concludes on the last business day before school resumes in September.

PROGRAM HOURS

7:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.

DAILY ACTIVITIES

Each day in the Summer Program includes:
- 45 minutes of martial arts training
- 1.5 hours of academics (reading, writing, math review, and related subjects)
- Group activities focused on social development
- Fun time, outdoor time (as scheduled), games, and structured social
  interaction

PROGRAM PHILOSOPHY

The Summer Program is designed to balance learning, martial arts, and fun
throughout the summer. Academic reinforcement prevents summer learning loss
while maintaining the physical and social development benefits of martial arts.

ENROLLMENT

Space in the Summer Program is very limited. Families are encouraged to inquire
early about availability.

TRIAL CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

TRIAL CLASS POLICY

The trial class is completely free with no commitment required. Prospective
students are welcome to come in and experience the training environment and
meet the instructors before making any decision about membership.

WHAT HAPPENS DURING A TRIAL CLASS

Students attend a regular scheduled class appropriate for their age group.
Children ages 4-12 attend a Children's Martial Arts class. Individuals 13 and
older attend an Adult Martial Arts class. Instructors observe student
performance during the trial and can assess whether a child who performs
exceptionally well might be a strong candidate for the adult class.

WHAT TO WEAR

Comfortable athletic clothing such as a t-shirt and shorts or athletic pants.
No special equipment or martial arts uniform is required for a trial class.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Martial arts training appropriate for the student's age group and intensity
level. For families interested in the Afterschool Program or Summer Program,
the trial visit is also an opportunity to speak with staff and instructors
directly about program structure, academic support, scheduling, and
availability.

================================================================================
REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR TRIAL CLASS BOOKING
================================================================================

The following information is required to book a trial class. This information
is collected for safety compliance, insurance requirements, and communication
purposes.

FULL NAME (FIRST AND LAST)

Full legal name is required to complete the student profile and register for
the trial class. The profile system requires first and last name to process
enrollment. Full legal names are also required on participation waivers under
state regulations.

EMAIL ADDRESS

Email address is required to complete the student profile and to send class
confirmations containing important details such as what to wear, when to
arrive, and what to expect. Email is also used for notifications about schedule
changes or cancellations. Contact information is kept private and secure and is
never shared with third parties.

PHONE NUMBER

Phone number is required to complete the student profile. Phone is used for
text reminders about upcoming classes, emergency contact if a student is
running late or cannot locate the facility, and last-minute communication
regarding schedule changes or cancellations. Liability insurance requires
emergency contact information for all participants.

DATE OF BIRTH

Date of birth is required to complete the student profile. The profile system
requires the actual date of birth — not just age — to be entered. Date of birth
is used to confirm age-appropriate program placement: Children's Martial Arts
for ages 4-12, Adult Martial Arts for ages 13 and up, and Health & Fitness for
adults only. Date of birth also satisfies insurance requirements and ensures
proper safety considerations are applied.

PARENT OR GUARDIAN INFORMATION (FOR STUDENTS UNDER 18)

For students under 18, a parent or guardian's information — including their
date of birth — is required because the minor's profile is linked to the parent
or guardian profile in the registration system. The parent or guardian profile
must be completed to register the minor for a trial class. State law requires
that minors cannot sign legal waivers independently; parent or guardian consent
is mandatory. Emergency medical decisions also require immediate parent or
guardian contact, and financial authorization for any future payments requires
parent or guardian information.

MEMBERSHIP & PRICING
================================================================================

MEMBERSHIP OVERVIEW

Universal Mixed Martial Arts offers membership options covering martial arts
training for children (ages 4-12) and adults (ages 13+), as well as the
Afterschool Program and Summer Program. Programs are structured to accommodate
different training goals, class frequency preferences, and family needs.

PRICING

Membership pricing varies based on several factors like the number of classes
per week a student would want to attend and their specific training goals.
Instructors will discuss all pricing options and membership plans during or
right after the free trial class. They will assess what program works best for
the student and provide accurate pricing for their specific situation. The
trial class is completely free with no commitment required.

AFTERSCHOOL AND SUMMER PROGRAM PRICING

Pricing for the Afterschool Program and Martial Arts Summer Program is
discussed with staff during a consultation or trial visit. These programs
include martial arts training as part of a broader academic and social
development package. Both programs are tax-deductible, and the academy
provides tax documentation for credit and deduction purposes.

FACTORS THAT INFORM PROGRAM RECOMMENDATIONS

Staff and instructors consider the following when discussing membership options:
- Age group (Children ages 4-12 or Adults ages 13+)
- Interest in martial arts only versus the Afterschool Program package
- Summer Program participation interest
- Interest in Health & Fitness classes (adults)
- Desired frequency of Weapons and Sparring class attendance
- Individual training goals and schedule preferences
- Birthday party rental interest
