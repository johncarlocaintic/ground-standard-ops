================================================================
GYM IDENTITY
================================================================

SOMA MVMT is a small-group adult fitness studio in Maplewood, New Jersey. The studio focuses on sustainable functional training for adults in their 30s through 50s.

Location: 6 West Parker Avenue, Maplewood, NJ 07040
Phone: (201) 403-3225
Email: info@somamvmt.com
Website: somamvmt.com
Timezone: Eastern (America/New_York)

================================================================
WHO TRAINS HERE
================================================================

Adult members. The studio's typical members are creative professionals and busy adults seeking a sustainable training environment.

================================================================
PROGRAMS
================================================================

Bookable for trial:
- SOMA MVMT Introduction Class (60 minutes)

Programs offered on the regular schedule (not directly trial-bookable; the introduction class is the entry point for any of these):
- Adult Small Group Fitness (Functional Strength Interval Training)
- Steel Mace Flow
- Mindfulness and Meditation
- Guided Breathwork

================================================================
THE INTRODUCTION CLASS
================================================================

The Introduction Class is the standard first session for new members. It is 60 minutes long. New members start here regardless of which regular class type they ultimately want to attend most.

================================================================
EQUIPMENT
================================================================

The studio uses kettlebells, steel maces, and bodyweight-based functional training equipment.

================================================================
TRAINING APPROACH
================================================================

The studio emphasizes sustainable training over high-intensity, burnout-driven workouts. Class sizes are small to allow individualized coaching attention.

================================================================
AGE BAND
================================================================

Adult studio. The studio does not currently run dedicated youth, kids, or teen programs.
