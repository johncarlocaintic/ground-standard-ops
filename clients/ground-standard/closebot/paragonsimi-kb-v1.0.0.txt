PROGRAMS
================================================================================

ADULT - BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU (GI): traditional uniform (the Gi). All-levels adult Gi classes through the week.

ADULT - BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU (NO-GI): practiced without the uniform, in rashguard and shorts/athletic pants. Multiple sessions through the week including a Saturday morning session.

ADULT - MUAY THAI: striking program. Full structured classes (warmup, technique, drilling, optional sparring) plus focused sessions and a sparring session.

ADULT - MIXED MARTIAL ARTS (MMA): blends striking and grappling.

FUN JITSU (ages 3-6): Jiu-Jitsu for young children.

KIDS JIU-JITSU (ages 7-14): structured kids Jiu-Jitsu program.

ADVANCED GI: for more advanced practitioners (not a beginner trial).

PRIVATE LESSONS: available upon request; details discussed with staff (not booked online).

TRIAL CLASS
================================================================================

New students can attend a free trial class to experience the training environment and instruction before committing. The trial class is completely free with no commitment. Loaner gear is available for trial students - no equipment purchase is required before a first class. During the trial, prospective members meet instructors, see how beginners are paired with experienced partners, and can ask staff about programs, goals, and membership.
