AGE POLICY
================================================================================

Kids program: ages 4 through 13
- Younger group: ages 4-7 (30-minute sessions)
- Older group: ages 8-13 (1-hour sessions)

Adult program: ages 14 and older
- Includes Adult No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu and Adult Striking (Muay Thai)
- Anyone 14 or older trains in adult classes, not the kids program

A prospective student who is 14 to 17 years old can attend adult classes but
must be enrolled with a parent or guardian involved in the sign-up. The
academy does not enroll unaccompanied minors through online booking; minors
without a parent involved are captured for staff follow-up.

================================================================================
PROGRAMS & CLASS TYPES
================================================================================

ADULT NO-GI BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU
For ages 14 and older. Practical, technique-based grappling with no traditional
Gi uniform. Welcoming to complete beginners and advanced grapplers alike.
Focus on control, calm under pressure, ground-based self-defense, and using
technique over strength. Booking calendar: "Adult Fundamentals BJJ".

ADULT STRIKING (MUAY THAI)
For ages 14 and older. Muay Thai, known as the Art of Eight Limbs, uses fists,
elbows, knees, and shins. Classes are welcoming to beginners while challenging
for experienced strikers. Whether the goal is fitness, self-defense, or
competition, training builds coordination, timing, and conditioning.
Booking calendar: "Adult Striking".

KIDS MARTIAL ARTS — AGES 4 TO 7
A combined martial arts program for younger kids ages 4 to 7 that blends
striking and Jiu-Jitsu. Sessions are 30 minutes. Classes are age-appropriate
and carefully supervised. Each class begins with warm-ups, transitions into
technique instruction, and concludes with games or drills that reinforce
lessons. Booking calendar: "Kids 4-7 Martial Arts".

KIDS MARTIAL ARTS — AGES 8 TO 13
A combined martial arts program for kids ages 8 to 13 that blends striking
and Jiu-Jitsu. Sessions are 1 hour. Classes are age-appropriate and carefully
supervised. Same instructional structure as the younger group but at a more
advanced pace and intensity. Booking calendar: "Kids 8-13 Martial Arts".

PRIVATE TRAINING (NOT BOOKABLE ONLINE)
Private one-on-one training is offered for both No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai.
Private sessions are personalized to the student's goals, allow faster progress
through direct coach feedback, and can be scheduled outside of regular group
class hours. Private training is a good fit for beginners who want extra
confidence before joining group classes, busy adults whose schedules don't fit
regular class times, competitors preparing for a tournament or fight, and kids
who thrive with individual attention.

Private training is NOT available for online booking. The right next step for
anyone interested in private training is to attend a free trial group class
first and discuss private training details directly with the coach in person.

OPEN MAT
Free rolling and open mat sessions are available after all adult Jiu-Jitsu
classes throughout the week. A dedicated Open Mat session also runs on Friday
evenings from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Open Mat is not a booked trial class; it is
a recurring drop-in session for current students and adults exploring the
academy.

WHAT KIDS LEARN
================================================================================

The kids program combines striking and Jiu-Jitsu to build confidence, focus,
discipline, and respect, while keeping kids active and having fun. Parents
report quick improvements: better behavior at home, more focus on schoolwork,
and healthier routines. Kids look forward to training and gain friendships
that support positive social development.

Benefits emphasized for kids:
- Confidence through skill mastery
- Focus and listening that carry over to school
- Respect for partners, instructors, and teamwork
- Discipline and accountability
- Healthy active outlet

The academy emphasizes control and responsibility — kids learn when and how
to use their skills appropriately. Classes are divided by age and sometimes
by experience level. Parents are welcome to stay and watch, especially during
a child's first classes.

no cost and no commitment.

What to bring:

program fit, and pricing in person based on the student's goals and the right
program for them.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: What martial arts does Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu offer?
A: Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu offers No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai
striking for adults, and a combined martial arts program for kids that blends
Jiu-Jitsu and striking across two age groups.

Q: Is Gi training available?
A: No. Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu focuses exclusively on No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu. No
traditional Gi uniform is required or used.

Q: What is the kids program structure?
A: The kids program is a combined martial arts curriculum that blends
Jiu-Jitsu and striking. Kids enroll into one of two age groups: ages 4-7 with
30-minute sessions, or ages 8-13 with 1-hour sessions. Kids are not split into
separate Jiu-Jitsu and striking enrollments — both are part of the same
program for their age group.

Q: What is the minimum age for adult classes?
A: Adult classes are for ages 14 and older. Anyone under 14 enrolls in the
kids program. Teens aged 14-17 can train in adult classes but must be enrolled
through a parent or guardian.

Q: Can I sign up my child for just Jiu-Jitsu or just striking?
A: The kids program is a combined curriculum, not separate enrollments. Kids
in the program get both Jiu-Jitsu and striking instruction across the week's
classes as part of one martial arts program.

Q: Do you offer private training?
A: Yes. Private training is available for both No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai
striking. Private sessions are one-on-one with a coach and are scheduled
outside of regular group class hours. Private training is not available for
online booking — the right next step is to attend a free trial group class
first and discuss private training details with the coach in person.

Q: Is there open mat available?
A: Yes. Free rolling and open mat are available after all adult Jiu-Jitsu
classes throughout the week. A dedicated Open Mat session also runs on
Friday evenings from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM.

Q: What should I bring to my first class?
A: Bring a water bottle and wear athletic clothing suitable for training.
Plan to arrive 10-15 minutes early to check in and meet the coach.

Q: Is the trial class actually free?
A: Yes. The trial class is completely free with no commitment required.

Q: When are classes scheduled?
A: Class times are managed through our booking system. Once contact info is
collected, the booking tool shows the available trial slots that match the
requested program and age group.

Q: Where is the gym located?
A: Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu is located at 1495 Lincoln Way East, Chambersburg,
PA 17201, serving Franklin County and surrounding areas including
Greencastle, Waynesboro, Shippensburg PA, and Hagerstown MD.

Q: How can I reach the gym?
A: The gym can be reached by phone at (717) 402-8999, or through the
website at https://www.masondixonjiujitsu.com.

Q: Will martial arts make my child aggressive?
A: No. The kids program emphasizes control, respect, and responsibility. Kids
learn when and how to use their skills appropriately.

Q: Can parents stay and watch class?
A: Yes. Parents are welcome to stay and watch, especially during a child's
first classes. Many parents find it reassuring to see how their child adapts
to training.
