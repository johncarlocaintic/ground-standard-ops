INTRODUCTORY CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

TRIAL / INTRO CLASS POLICY:
New prospects may attend any regularly scheduled class as their introductory
class. There are no separate new student time slots — all classes on the
schedule are open to introductory attendance.

The academy trains students at all levels, from complete beginners with zero
prior experience to current professional athletes. New students do not need
prior martial arts experience to attend.

WHAT TO BRING FOR FIRST CLASS:
- Comfortable athletic clothing (shorts and t-shirt, or athletic pants)
- Water bottle recommended
- No equipment required for the introductory class

WHAT TO EXPECT:
Classes are conducted in a structured training environment. Beginners are
integrated into regular classes alongside more experienced students.
