BUSINESS INFORMATION
================================================================================

Business Name: Universal Mixed Martial Arts
Address: 114-10 Liberty Ave, South Richmond Hill, NY 11419
Phone: (718) 659-1700
Website: https://www.universalmixedmartialarts.com

Universal Mixed Martial Arts is a martial arts academy in South Richmond Hill, NY offering martial arts training for children and adults, health and fitness classes, an afterschool program with academic support, and a summer program.

================================================================================
PROGRAMS & CLASSES
================================================================================

CHILDREN'S MARTIAL ARTS (Ages 4-12)
45-minute classes teaching a comprehensive martial arts curriculum at an age-appropriate intensity level. All children's classes accommodate beginners.

ADULT MARTIAL ARTS (Ages 13+)
45-minute classes teaching the same martial arts curriculum as the children's program at a significantly higher intensity level. There is no upper age limit. Students starting at age 13 join this program. New students are welcome.

HEALTH & FITNESS (Adults Only)
Exercise-based classes for adults who want a challenging fitness experience without focusing on martial arts technique - cardio, strength training, yoga, and H.I.I.T.

WEAPONS CLASS (Children & Adults Combined)
A combined class covering weapons training.

SPARRING CLASS (Children & Adults Combined)
A combined class focusing on sparring techniques and application.

AFTERSCHOOL PROGRAM
A structured after-school service prioritizing academic homework assistance alongside martial arts training, with extended hours and school-closure-day coverage to support working parents.

CURRICULUM NOTE
The children's and adult martial arts programs teach the same curriculum; the difference is intensity. Adult classes are considerably more intense. High-performing children's students may be moved to adult classes at the instructor's discretion.

================================================================================
TRIAL CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

New students start with a free trial class with no commitment. New students are welcome to join the regularly scheduled classes. The trial is the starting point before discussing membership.

================================================================================
MEMBERSHIP & PRICING
================================================================================

Membership pricing varies based on the program and the student's goals. Instructors discuss all pricing options and membership plans during or right after the free trial class and provide accurate pricing for the individual's situation. The trial class is completely free with no commitment.

A: Children's Martial Arts is for ages 4-12. Adult Martial Arts is for ages 13 and up with no upper age limit.

A: No. All classes accommodate beginners and new students are welcome to join regularly scheduled classes.

Q: What is the difference between the children's and adult classes?
A: They teach the same curriculum. The difference is intensity - adult classes are considerably more intense.

Q: Do you have an afterschool program?
A: Yes. The Afterschool Program prioritizes homework assistance alongside martial arts training, with extended hours and school-closure coverage.

Q: Where are you located?
A: 114-10 Liberty Ave, South Richmond Hill, NY 11419.

Q: Do you offer health and fitness classes?
A: Yes. Health & Fitness is an adults-only exercise-based class (cardio, strength, yoga, H.I.I.T.) without a martial arts technique focus.
