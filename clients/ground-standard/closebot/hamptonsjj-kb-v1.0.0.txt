and backgrounds in the Hamptons community. HJJ was built on the belief that
training should not be locked into a single style or team — students here
benefit from multiple lineages, different teaching methods, and a supportive,
inclusive community.

PROGRAMS & CLASS TYPES
================================================================================

ADULT BRAZILIAN Jiu-Jitsu
For ages 13 and older. HJJ offers the full spectrum of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu
from beginner Fundamentals to advanced training, covering both Gi and No-Gi
formats. The Fundamentals program is the starting point for new students and
is designed for those with no prior BJJ experience. Classes are welcoming to
complete beginners and advanced practitioners alike. The focus is on
technique, leverage, and strategy — the art of using skill over strength.
Booking calendar: "Adult BJJ".

ADULT MUAY THAI
For ages 13 and older. Muay Thai, also known as the Art of Eight Limbs,
uses punches, kicks, elbows, and knees. HJJ's striking program is designed
for all levels from fitness-focused beginners to serious practitioners. HJJ
also offers Judo, Wrestling, and MMA classes that complement the Muay Thai
program (see Non-Bookable Programs below for details).
Booking calendar: "Adult Muay Thai".

YOUTH Jiu-Jitsu — AGES 4 TO 7
Jiu-Jitsu-focused classes for children ages 4 to 7. Sessions are
age-appropriate and supervised, building foundational movement patterns,
confidence, and basic technique in a fun and structured environment.
Hamptons JJ runs the largest youth martial arts program in the Hamptons.
Booking calendar: "Kids 4-7 BJJ".

YOUTH Jiu-Jitsu — AGES 8 TO 12
Jiu-Jitsu-focused classes for kids ages 8 to 12. Classes are more
technically advanced than the younger group and build on fundamentals with
more complex technique work, drilling, and live training at an age-appropriate
pace. Booking calendar: "Kids 8-12 BJJ".

================================================================================
NON-BOOKABLE PROGRAMS
================================================================================

PRIVATE LESSONS
One-on-one private training is available at both the Southampton and
Westhampton Beach locations. Private sessions are personalized to the
student's specific goals — whether that is accelerating fundamentals,
competition prep, technique refinement, or a schedule that does not fit
group classes. Private lessons are not available for online booking. The
best next step for anyone interested in private lessons is to attend a free
trial group class first and speak with the instructor in person.

TRX GROUP CLASSES (HAMPTONS POWERED BY TRX)
HJJ offers a functional fitness program powered by TRX — a suspension
training system that uses adjustable straps and bodyweight to build
strength, conditioning, and movement. Sessions are led by certified
instructors and are designed to complement martial arts training. TRX classes
are not currently available for online booking through this system. Contact
the gym directly at (631) 900-2780 to ask about TRX class availability and
scheduling.

JUDO, WRESTLING, AND MMA
HJJ offers Judo, Wrestling, and MMA classes as part of its broader martial
arts programming. These classes are not currently available as separate online
bookings. The free trial class is the best starting point — students can
explore the full range of programs and discuss which disciplines fit their
goals with the instructor during or after the trial.

WHAT KIDS LEARN
================================================================================

The youth programs at HJJ build confidence, focus, discipline, and respect
while keeping kids active and engaged. HJJ's youth program is the largest in
the Hamptons, reflecting the quality and consistency that has earned the trust
of families in the community.

Key benefits:
- Confidence that builds as skills develop
- Focus and listening that transfer to school performance
- Respect for partners, teammates, and instructors
- Discipline and follow-through
- Active, healthy outlet in a structured and supervised environment

Parents are welcome to watch classes, especially during a child's first
visits. The youth programs use Jiu-Jitsu technique and movement as the
vehicle for building these broader life skills.

INSTRUCTORS & TEAM
================================================================================

GREG MELITA — Founder & Head Instructor
Greg Melita is the founder of Hamptons Jiu-Jitsu and has over 20 years of
experience in Jiu-Jitsu and other combat sports. He has developed a deep
understanding of technique, strategy, and the pedagogy of martial arts
instruction. Greg built HJJ around the vision of offering students access
to multiple lineages and teaching styles rather than locking them into a
single approach.

THE COACHING TEAM
HJJ brings together coaches from different Jiu-Jitsu lineages, styles, and
teams. The team includes Black Belts and coaches with diverse competitive and
teaching backgrounds. Coaches include James Lispesa, Randy Nieves, Josiah
Tyte, Richard Byrne, Dan Curro, and Howard Greenberg, alongside Greg Melita.

This multi-lineage approach is intentional — students at HJJ are exposed to
a broader range of techniques and perspectives than they would find at most
single-affiliation academies.

WHAT TO EXPECT — FIRST CLASS (FREE TRIAL)
================================================================================

The entry point for any new student is a free trial class. The trial has no
cost and no commitment required.

What to bring:
- Water bottle
- Athletic clothing appropriate for training (no uniform required for the
  trial class)

Arrival: Plan to arrive a few minutes early to get checked in and introduced
to the instructor before class begins.

After the trial: The instructor will walk through membership options, program
fit, and pricing in person based on the student's goals and the best program
for them.

================================================================================

Class schedules are managed through the live booking system. Specific class
