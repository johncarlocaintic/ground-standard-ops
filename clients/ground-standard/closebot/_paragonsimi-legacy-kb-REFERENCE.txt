REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR TRIAL CLASS BOOKING
================================================================================

The following information is collected from all new students to complete their
student profile and register for a trial class. Each field is required for
safety, legal compliance, and communication purposes.

1. FULL NAME (First and Last)
   Full legal name is required to create the student profile in the registration
   system, which requires both first and last name. Full legal name also satisfies
   liability insurance requirements and is needed for legal waiver documentation,
   as state regulations require full legal names on participation waivers.

2. EMAIL ADDRESS
   Email is required to complete the student profile and send class confirmation
   details, including what to wear, when to arrive, and what to expect. Email is
   also used for notifications about schedule changes or cancellations. Contact
   information is kept private and secure and is never shared with third parties.

3. PHONE NUMBER
   Phone number is required to complete the student profile. It is used for
   text reminders about classes, emergency contact if a student is running late
   or unable to locate the facility, and last-minute communication for schedule
   changes. Liability insurance also requires emergency contact information for
   all participants.

4. DATE OF BIRTH
   The actual date of birth — not age — is required because the profile system
   needs it to be entered directly. Date of birth confirms appropriate program
   placement (Fun Jitsu for ages 3–6, Kids/Youth Jiu-Jitsu for ages 8–12+,
   Adult programs for older students), supports safety considerations, and
   meets insurance requirements.

5. PARENT OR GUARDIAN INFORMATION (Required if student is under 18)
   For minors, parent or guardian information — including the parent/guardian's
   date of birth — is required because the child's profile is attached to the
   parent/guardian profile in the registration system. State law requires that
   minors cannot sign legal waivers independently; a parent or guardian must
   provide consent. Emergency medical decisions and financial authorization
   for payments also require parent or guardian involvement.
