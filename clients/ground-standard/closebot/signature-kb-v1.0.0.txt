CLASSES & PROGRAMS
================================================================================

ADULTS - BRAZILIAN Jiu-Jitsu (GI AND NO-GI)
Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes for adults are available in both Gi and No-Gi formats. New students start with the BJJ Beginners class. Introductory classes for new students are available by appointment, Monday through Friday between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Each introductory session is 45 minutes.

WOMEN'S CLASS - BRAZILIAN Jiu-Jitsu
A dedicated women's Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class runs every Friday from 5:30 PM to 6:30 PM, led by Yasmin Santana. The class focuses on self-defense techniques in a supportive environment.

KIDS - BRAZILIAN Jiu-Jitsu
Children's BJJ classes are divided into three age-based groups:
- Kids 1: Ages 5-7
- Kids 2: Ages 8-10
- Kids 3: Ages 11-14

Kids classes run Monday through Friday afternoons and Saturday mornings.

PRIVATE LESSONS
Private lessons are available at all levels. Scheduling and rates are discussed with staff.

================================================================================
TRIAL CLASS & FIRST CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

Trial classes are available for new students. Students may sign up via www.sofjj.com or directly at the academy.

- Class duration: 45 minutes
- Students are introduced to basic Jiu-Jitsu fundamentals and two self-defense techniques
- Uniforms are provided for intro classes
- No equipment required for the first class
- Water is available; students may bring their own bottle
- Students are asked to arrive 10 minutes early

================================================================================
MEMBERSHIP & PRICING
================================================================================

Membership pricing varies based on training goals and schedule. Instructors discuss all pricing options during or right after the free trial class. The trial class is completely free with no commitment required.

Family, military, first responder, and student discounts are available. Specific amounts are discussed with staff.

No additional fees apply beyond membership cost. Card payments are accepted.

Drop-in attendance is available; rates are discussed with staff.

================================================================================
POLICIES
================================================================================

Cancellation: 30 days' notice required, no cancellation fee.

Memberships can be paused for vacations or other reasons.

Belt promotions: Kids promotions typically held once per year. Adult promotions follow IBJJF rules.

Zero-tolerance policy for bullying, harassment, or abusive behavior.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What martial arts does Signature of Jiu-Jitsu teach?
A: Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in both Gi and No-Gi formats for adults, women, and children.
