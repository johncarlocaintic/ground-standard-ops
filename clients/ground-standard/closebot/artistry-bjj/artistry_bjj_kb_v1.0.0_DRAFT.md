## SECTION 5 — TRIAL / FIRST CLASS INFORMATION


A 1-week free trial is available for new students.

New students are welcome to show up to any regular class. The instructor is
made aware of new students and will partner them with a more experienced
student. The technique of the day is modified to something more accessible for
beginners. Sparring is optional and never required for new students.

No equipment is needed for the first class. Wear comfortable clothes with no
pockets or zippers.

Guests are welcome to watch a class before deciding to join.

Formal membership sign-up is done in person at the gym.


## SECTION 6 — MEMBERSHIP AND PRICING


Membership pricing is not published. Pricing and membership options are
discussed with new students following the free trial.

Monthly memberships are paid by card. Yearly memberships are paid by cash.

A one-time sign-up fee applies. The sign-up fee includes gym gear.

Membership can be paused with 30 days' notice. A doctor's note allows an
exception to the 30-day pause notice requirement.

Cancellation requires 30 days' notice. No cancellation fee specified.

Family discount: Buy-two-get-two for members within the same household.

Discounts available for military and first responders.
