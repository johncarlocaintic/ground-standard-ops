PROGRAMS OFFERED
================================================================================

ALL-LEVEL NO-GI Jiu-Jitsu
Open to all skill levels. Covers both fundamental and advanced concepts. New
students with some prior experience are welcome in All-Level classes.

FUNDAMENTAL NO-GI
The recommended starting point for beginners. Covers the core building blocks
of No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu in a structured, approachable environment. Runs Monday
through Friday at 6:00 PM. This is the best class for first-timers and those
new to grappling.

PRO CLASS (ADVANCED)
An advanced-level session for experienced grapplers. Attendance is
invitation-based and limited to advanced students only. A $50 mat fee applies
for the Pro Class — this session is not included in the free trial offer.

WRESTLING
Available Friday evenings at 7:00 PM. Open to members.

COMMUNITY OPEN MAT (SUNDAY)
Every Sunday at 12:00 PM. Open to members and grapplers from all gyms in the
area. No affiliation requirement.

KIDS PROGRAM (COMING SOON)
A dedicated kids program is in development and will be coached by Anthony
Salvatore. Not currently active. Interested families can contact the academy
for updates.

WOMEN'S ONLY CLASSES (FUTURE)
Women's only classes are planned for the future. Not currently active. Women
are welcome in all regular classes now.

PRIVATE LESSONS
Private lessons are available. Each instructor sets their own rate. Contact
Drew Moreno at (512) 612-6200 or email info@simplemanmartialarts.com for
availability and pricing.

TRIAL CLASS INFORMATION
================================================================================

Free Trial Availability:
A free trial class is available for all programs except the Pro Class.
Beginners and visitors may attend at no cost with no commitment required.

Pro Class Exception:
The Pro Class requires a $50 mat fee for trial attendees. This session is
advanced and invitation-based.

Recommended Trial Class for Beginners:
The Fundamental No-Gi class (Monday–Friday at 6:00 PM) is the recommended
first class for anyone new to Jiu-Jitsu or grappling.

Recommended Trial Class for Experienced Grapplers:
Those with prior martial arts or grappling experience are welcome to attend
any All-Level No-Gi class for their trial.

Observation:
Anyone is welcome to watch a class before deciding to join. No appointment
is needed to observe.

Tours:
Tours of the facility are available any time during business hours. No
appointment required.

What to Bring for a Trial Class:
- Rashguard and athletic shorts (no zippers, pockets discouraged)
- Water bottle
- Towel
- Change of clothes (showers are available on-site)
- No gear or uniform required for trial

No Uniform Required for Trial:
Regular members wear rashguard and shorts. Gear is available for purchase
in the gym.

CULTURE & TRAINING ENVIRONMENT
================================================================================

Simple Man Martial Arts is a family-first, inclusive environment. The academy
is welcoming to all ages, sizes, and skill levels. Zero tolerance for
bullying or harassment. Students are expected to maintain personal hygiene
(clean nails, clean gear, clean clothing) and respect the training environment.

"We are a family. We treat everyone with respect."

The Sunday Community Open Mat at 12:00 PM is open to members of all gyms in
the area, reflecting the academy's community-driven philosophy.

Instructor Philosophy: World-class Jiu-Jitsu instruction focused on safe,
technical training, mutual respect, and continuous improvement.

Belt and rank promotions are based on instructor discretion and live training
observation. There is no fixed testing schedule.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: What martial art does Simple Man Martial Arts teach?
A: Simple Man Martial Arts specializes in No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Programs
include All-Level No-Gi, Fundamental No-Gi, Pro Class, and Wrestling.

Q: Do I need experience to join?
A: No experience is required. The Fundamental No-Gi class (Mon–Fri at 6:00 PM)
is designed for beginners and is the recommended starting point for anyone new
to Jiu-Jitsu.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes. A free trial class is available for all programs except the Pro Class.
The Pro Class requires a $50 mat fee for visitors.

Q: What class should I try first?
A: Beginners are recommended to attend the Fundamental No-Gi class, Monday
through Friday at 6:00 PM. Those with prior grappling experience are welcome
to try any All-Level No-Gi class.

Q: How much does a membership cost?
A: Membership pricing varies based on several factors like the number of
classes per week you'd want to attend and your specific training goals.
Instructors will discuss all the pricing options and membership plans with you
during or right after your free trial class. They'll be able to assess what
program works best for you and provide accurate pricing for your situation.
The trial class is completely free with no commitment required.

Q: Is there a drop-in option?
A: Drop-in and membership options will be discussed with you by an instructor
during or right after your free trial class. Pricing varies based on your
training goals and schedule. The trial class is completely free with no
commitment required.

Q: Are there any discounts available?
A: Discount options and membership plans will be covered with you by an
instructor during or right after your free trial class. The trial class is
completely free with no commitment required.

Q: What do I need to bring to my first class?
A: Bring a rashguard, athletic shorts (no zippers), a water bottle, a towel,
and a change of clothes. No equipment or uniform is required for the trial.

Q: Is there a uniform requirement?
A: No Gi uniform is required. Members wear rashguard and shorts. No traditional
gi is used in training.

Q: Can I watch a class before joining?
A: Yes. Anyone is welcome to observe a class during regular hours. No
appointment needed.

Q: Can I take a tour of the facility?
A: Yes. Tours are available any time during business hours. No appointment
required.

Q: Do you have locker rooms and showers?
A: Yes. Locker rooms and on-site showers are available. Bring a towel.

Q: What is the cancellation policy?
A: There is no cancellation fee. Memberships are month-to-month and can be
paused or cancelled at any time.

Q: Can I pause my membership?
A: Yes. Memberships can be paused for vacations, illness, or other
circumstances.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: Credit and debit cards only. Simple Man Martial Arts is a cashless facility.

Q: Is the Pro Class open to beginners?
A: No. The Pro Class is an advanced, invitation-based session. Beginners
should start with the Fundamental No-Gi class.

Q: Do you offer private lessons?
A: Yes. Private lessons are available. Each instructor sets their own rate.
Contact the academy at info@simplemanmartialarts.com or Drew Moreno at
(512) 612-6200 for availability.

Q: Is there a kids program?
A: A Kids Program is coming soon and will be coached by Anthony Salvatore.
It is not currently active. Contact the academy for updates on launch timing.

Q: Are there women's only classes?
A: Women's only classes are planned for the future but not yet active. Women
are welcome in all current classes.

Q: What is the Sunday Open Mat?
A: Every Sunday at 12:00 PM, Simple Man Martial Arts hosts a Community Open
Mat open to members and grapplers from all gyms. No affiliation required.

Q: Is there parking at the facility?
A: Yes. Ample parking is available at the location.

Q: Is filming allowed in the gym?
A: Filming requires prior approval from a staff member.

Q: Who do I contact for general inquiries?
A: Email info@simplemanmartialarts.com or contact General Manager Drew Moreno
at (512) 612-6200 during business hours.
