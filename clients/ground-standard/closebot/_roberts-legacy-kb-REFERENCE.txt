Name: Roberts Family MMA
Industry: Martial Arts / Mixed Martial Arts Training
Location: 25 New Athol Road, Orange, MA

Massachusetts. The academy offers training in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Thai Boxing, and

PROGRAMS & CLASSES
================================================================================

KIDS BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU (Ages 5–17)
Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training designed for children ages 5 to 17. Classes are held
Monday and Wednesday from 5:15–6:15 PM.

KIDS STRIKING (Ages 5–17)
Striking-based martial arts training for children. Tuesday classes run as a combined
group for Ages 5–17, 5:15–6:00 PM. Thursday classes are divided into two concurrent
age-specific sessions: Ages 5–10 and Ages 11–17, both running 5:15–6:00 PM.

ADULT THAI BOXING
Thai Boxing classes for adults. Available Monday and Wednesday, 6:15–7:15 PM.

ADULT BOXING
Boxing classes for adults. Available Tuesday and Thursday, 6:15–7:15 PM.

ADULT FUNDAMENTALS BJJ (GI)
Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu fundamentals class for adult students. Available Monday and
Wednesday, 7:30–8:30 PM.

ADULT ALL-LEVELS BJJ (GI)
Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class open to adult students of all levels. Available Monday
and Wednesday, 8:30–9:30 PM.

ADULT FUNDAMENTALS BJJ (NO-GI)
No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu fundamentals for adult students. Available Tuesday and
Thursday, 7:30–8:30 PM.

ADULT ALL-LEVELS BJJ (NO-GI)
No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class open to adult students of all levels. Available
Tuesday and Thursday, 8:30–9:30 PM.

OPEN TRAINING
Friday open mat session available to members. Runs 5:30–7:30 PM.

FEMALE ONLY JIU-JITSU (All Ages)
A dedicated jiu-jitsu class for females of all ages. Available every Sunday,
2:00–3:00 PM.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Where is Roberts Family MMA located?
A: Roberts Family MMA is located at 25 New Athol Road, Orange, MA.

Q: What classes are available for kids?
A: Kids Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (Ages 5–17) is held Monday and Wednesday from
5:15–6:15 PM. Kids Striking is held Tuesday from 5:15–6:00 PM for Ages 5–17
as a combined group. On Thursday, Kids Striking splits into two concurrent
age-specific sessions: one for Ages 5–10 and one for Ages 11–17, both from
5:15–6:00 PM.

Q: What adult classes are available?
A: Adult classes include Thai Boxing (Monday and Wednesday, 6:15–7:15 PM),
Boxing (Tuesday and Thursday, 6:15–7:15 PM), Gi BJJ Fundamentals and All-Levels
(Monday and Wednesday, 7:30–8:30 PM and 8:30–9:30 PM), and No-Gi BJJ
Fundamentals and All-Levels (Tuesday and Thursday, 7:30–8:30 PM and 8:30–9:30 PM).
Friday Open Training runs 5:30–7:30 PM.

Q: Is there a class specifically for women or females?
A: Yes. Female Only Jiu-Jitsu (All Ages) is held every Sunday from 2:00–3:00 PM.

Q: What is Open Training on Friday?
A: Open Training is held every Friday from 5:30–7:30 PM. It is an open mat
session for members.

Q: Are there classes on Saturday?
A: No classes are currently scheduled on Saturday.

Q: Does the academy offer both Gi and No-Gi BJJ?
A: Yes. Gi BJJ classes (Fundamentals and All-Levels) are held Monday and Wednesday.
No-Gi BJJ classes (Fundamentals and All-Levels) are held Tuesday and Thursday.

Q: What is the difference between Fundamentals and All-Levels BJJ classes?
A: Fundamentals classes are designed to build core techniques and are suited for
newer students. All-Levels classes are open to students at any experience level.
Both formats are offered in Gi and No-Gi tracks.

Q: How much does membership cost?
A: Membership pricing varies based on several factors like the number of classes
per week you'd want to attend and your specific training goals. Instructors will
discuss all the pricing options and membership plans with you during or right after
your free trial class. They'll be able to assess what program works best for you
and provide accurate pricing for your situation. The trial class is completely free
with no commitment required.

Q: What gear do I need for Thai Boxing classes?
A: Hand wraps, gloves, and shin pads are required for Adult Thai Boxing.

Q: What gear do I need for Boxing classes?
A: Hand wraps and gloves are required for Adult Boxing.

Q: Can I buy gear at the academy?
A: Yes. Gear is available for purchase on-site. Hand wraps are $10. Other gear
is also available at the academy.

Q: Where can I view the class schedule or book a class online?
A: The full schedule and booking are available at:
https://bjjlink.com/pub/academy/6657f9835219b60c1adf2f9e/schedule?flat=true

Q: I have more questions about programs, pricing, or which class is right for me.
A: Staff and instructors at the academy are the best resource for those specifics.
Coming in for a first class is the most straightforward way to get all questions
answered, see the facility, and find the right program fit.
