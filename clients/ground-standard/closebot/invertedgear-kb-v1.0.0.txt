================================================================================
SAFETY & CULTURE
================================================================================

Safety is a core value. Students learn from day one that protecting themselves and their partners is a shared responsibility; aggressive or reckless behavior is not tolerated. Most training is done through controlled positional games. Coaches match students by size, strength, experience, and temperament. Tapping is taught as part of learning. Mats are disinfected at least twice a day. Beginners are never forced to spar and can modify sparring to their comfort level.

Kids classes have extra coaches with small-group attention; the student-to-instructor ratio is 6:1 for kids and 10:1 for adults. Instructors are SafeSport trained and certified, upholding national standards for a safe, respectful environment, especially for minors. Zero tolerance for bullying or harassment. Wheelchair-accessible entrance via ramp. The academy works with families on allergy and medical accommodations.

================================================================================
BELT PROMOTIONS
================================================================================

Kids belt promotions happen every month. Adult belt promotions happen every 6 months. Promotions are based on time on the mat, attendance, attitude, and technical development. Stripes are awarded along the way.
