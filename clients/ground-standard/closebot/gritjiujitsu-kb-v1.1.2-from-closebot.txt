PROGRAMS OVERVIEW
================================================================================

BRAZILIAN Jiu-Jitsu (JitZ)

Grit offers BJJ in both Gi and No-Gi formats. Classes are structured into
distinct levels so students train with peers at a compatible skill level.
The Fundamental track is designed for beginners and covers foundational
techniques and positions. Intermediate and Advance/Competition tracks
build on that foundation for students progressing in their training.
The Breakfast Club and Lunch Rollz sessions offer additional mat time
for adults outside of evening hours. Open Mat on Saturdays is available
for all students to roll freely and practice techniques.

TEENS JIT (Ages 13-15)

A dedicated class track for teenagers that runs parallel to the adult
evening schedule. Teens attend Monday through Thursday evenings and
have access to a Friday session as well.

WOMENS JITZ

A dedicated Jiu-Jitsu session for women, held every Wednesday evening.
This class runs at the same time as other evening sessions, providing
a focused environment for women who prefer training together.

MUAY THAI

Muay Thai is offered at Fundamental and Inter/Advance levels for adults.
The Fundamental classes run multiple sessions per evening on weekdays,
allowing students to choose a session that fits their schedule. The
Inter/Advance class runs on Wednesday evenings and Friday afternoons.
Monday's adult Muay Thai schedule includes intro-to-sparring and sparring
blocks in sequence. All sparring classes require personal protection:
mouth guard and shin guards are mandatory.

KIDS MUAY THAI (Ages 5 to 13)

Short-format Muay Thai classes for children run on Monday, Wednesday,
and Friday. These sessions are designed to introduce young students
to striking fundamentals in a safe, structured environment.

REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR TRIAL CLASS BOOKING
================================================================================

To book a trial class, the following information is collected for safety,
liability, and communication purposes.

REQUIRED FIELDS:
  1. Full Name (First and Last)
  2. Email Address
  3. Phone Number
  4. Date of Birth
  5. Parent or Guardian Information (required if student is under 18)

WHY THIS INFORMATION IS COLLECTED:

Full legal name is required to create a student profile in the registration
system and is also necessary for liability waiver completion on the first day.

Email address is used to send class confirmation details, schedule reminders,
and any communication regarding the booked session. Email is kept private and
not shared with third parties.

Phone number is used for text or call reminders about the scheduled class
and for last-minute communication regarding any schedule changes.

Date of birth is required to complete the student profile and to confirm
correct program placement. Kids programs (ages 4-13), Teens (ages 13-15),
and Adult programs (ages 14 and up) have different class tracks, and date
of birth ensures each student is placed in the appropriate class.

Parent or guardian information is required for students under 18 because
minors cannot sign legal waivers independently. A parent or guardian must
provide consent, and their profile is linked to the child's record in
the registration system.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: What martial arts does Grit Jiu-Jitsu & Muay Thai offer?
A: Grit offers Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai. BJJ is available in
   Gi and No-Gi formats across Fundamental, Intermediate, and Advance
   levels. Muay Thai is offered at Fundamental and Inter/Advance levels.
   Both disciplines are available for kids, teens, and adults.

Q: What age can my child start training?
A: Kids BJJ starts at age 4 and up. Kids Muay Thai starts at age 5
   and up. Both programs run Monday through Friday with age-appropriate
   class times. Teens (ages 13-15) have their own dedicated JitZ track.

Q: Do I need any experience to join?
A: No prior experience is required. Fundamental classes in both BJJ and
   Muay Thai are designed for complete beginners. New students start with
   introductory classes regardless of background.

Q: Is there a free trial class available?
A: Introductory trial classes are available. New students follow the
   Intro Schedule based on their age and chosen discipline. Specific
   trial class policies and availability are confirmed by contacting
   staff directly.

Q: What do I need to bring to my first class?
A: Comfortable athletic clothing such as shorts and a t-shirt or athletic
   pants. No shoes are worn on the mats. No equipment is needed for
   introductory and fundamental classes. Sparring classes require a
   mouth guard and shin guards, but those are not required for first visits.

Q: How much does membership cost?
A: Membership pricing varies based on several factors like the number of
   classes per week you'd want to attend and your specific training goals.
   Instructors will discuss all the pricing options and membership plans
   with you during or right after your free trial class. They'll be able
   to assess what program works best for you and provide accurate pricing
   for your situation. The trial class is completely free with no
   commitment required.

Q: What is the schedule for beginners?
A: Kids BJJ Fundamental runs Monday through Thursday from 5:00pm to 5:30pm
   for ages 4-13. Adult BJJ Fundamental runs Monday through Thursday from
   6:30pm to 7:30pm for ages 14 and up. Kids Muay Thai intro runs Monday
   at 4:30pm and Wednesday at 5:00pm. Adult Muay Thai intro runs Monday
   at 5:00pm and Tuesday through Thursday starting at 5:30pm.

Q: Are classes available in the morning?
A: Yes. The Breakfast Club JitZ session runs Monday, Thursday, and Friday
   from 6:00am to 7:00am. A Lunch Rollz session is also available on
   Wednesdays from 11:30am to 12:30pm.

Q: Is there a women-only class?
A: Yes. Women's JitZ is offered every Wednesday from 5:30pm to 6:30pm.

Q: What is Open Mat?
A: Open Mat is a free-rolling session held every Saturday from 9:00am to
   10:00am. It is an opportunity for students to practice techniques and
   roll without structured instruction.

Q: Do you offer No-Gi BJJ?
A: Yes. No-Gi classes are held on Wednesday and Thursday evenings for
   the KidZ JitZ Intermediate, KidZ JitZ Advance/Competition, and Adult
   JitZ Inter/Advance tracks.

Q: What gear is required for sparring?
A: All sparring classes require a mouth guard and shin guards as personal
   protection. These are not required for Fundamental or introductory classes.

Q: Can I watch a class before signing up?
A: Specific observation policies are confirmed by contacting staff directly.

Q: Is Grit right for someone focused on competition?
A: A KidZ JitZ Advance/Competition track exists for younger students with
   competitive goals. Adult competitive training options are best discussed
   with instructors during the trial class visit.

Q: How do I book my first class?
A: Booking is done by contacting Grit directly via their website.
