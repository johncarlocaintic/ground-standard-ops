PROGRAMS & CLASS DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

KIDS PROGRAM (Ages 5-12) - "Little Hammer"
The Little Hammer program serves children ages 5 to 12, with classes structured to develop foundational martial arts skills. Kids classes include Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Wrestling, MMA, and Kickboxing across the week.

TEEN PROGRAM (Ages 13 through high school)
A dedicated teen martial arts program for ages 13 through high school.

YOUTH & HIGH SCHOOL WRESTLING
Foundational and competitive wrestling instruction for school-age athletes, including a high school wrestling group and a Girls Only Wrestling class.

BRAZILIAN Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)
BJJ training is available in both Gi and No-Gi formats. Gi BJJ is trained wearing the traditional uniform. No-Gi BJJ is practiced in shorts and a rash guard without the traditional uniform. Open Mat sessions are unstructured training time for members to drill and roll.

KICKBOXING / MUAY THAI
Kickboxing classes are available for all levels throughout the week. Muay Thai training is also offered. On the gym's class-scheduling platform, Muay Thai classes are listed under the Kickboxing category.

MMA (Mixed Martial Arts)
MMA classes blend striking and grappling disciplines and are available multiple times per week.

KETTLEBELL / CONDITIONING
Kettlebell classes are athletic conditioning sessions for all fitness levels.

NO-GI FUNDAMENTALS
No-Gi grappling classes run multiple times per week across morning and evening slots, suitable for all levels developing grappling skills without the traditional Gi.

OPEN MAT
Unstructured training sessions where members can drill techniques and roll freely.

================================================================================
GYM STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Hammer Sports & Performance serves two types of training members: competitive fighters (fight team) and hobbyist practitioners. All membership types include gym access.

Average class sizes range from approximately 5 students in morning sessions to 15 students in evening sessions, with an average of 8 to 12 students per class.

No live sparring is conducted during introductory sessions. Intro attendees participate in technique and pad work only, alongside regular class participants.

================================================================================
MEMBERSHIP & PRICING
================================================================================

Hammer Sports & Performance offers multiple membership tiers covering Kids, Teen, Conditioning, Unlimited, and Ultimate (with private-session add-ons). All memberships include gym access.

Membership pricing varies based on factors like the number of classes per week and your specific training goals. Instructors discuss all pricing options and membership plans with you during or right after your free trial class, and will assess what program fits best. The trial class is free with no commitment required.
