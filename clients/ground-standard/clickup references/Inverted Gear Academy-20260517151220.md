**What safety measures are in place during training?**
At Inverted Gear Academy, several safety measures are in place to make sure everyone — from kids to adults — can train hard while minimizing risk:

**Mat Culture and Expectations**: Safety is a core value. Students are taught from day one that protecting themselves and their training partners is a shared responsibility. Aggressive or reckless behavior is not tolerated.

**Instructor Supervision**: Classes are always supervised by experienced instructors (Nelson, Hillary, and a few trusted assistants). Coaches monitor the room, correct risky behavior quickly, and adjust pairings when needed.

**Games-Based Approach**: Most of the training is done through controlled positional games, which limit dangerous scrambles and encourage thoughtful movement instead of chaotic brawling.

**Partner Matching**: Coaches actively match students by size, strength, experience level, and temperament to keep training as safe as possible.

**Tap Early, Tap Often**: Students are reminded constantly that tapping is part of the learning process. There’s no shame in tapping — only respect for your own body and your training partners.

**Hygiene and Clean Mats**: Mats are cleaned daily. Students are expected to show up with clean gear, trimmed nails, and good hygiene to prevent skin infections and illnesses.

**Gi and No-Gi Safety Rules**: In gi classes, grip fighting is kept controlled, with no yanking or dangerous pulls.

**In no-gi classes**, submissions like heel hooks are reserved for advanced students and taught under strict supervision.

**Kids Classes Supervision**: Kids' sessions have extra coaches on the mat, with small group attention to ensure play stays safe, constructive, and fun.

**Optional Sparring for Beginners**: Beginners are never forced to spar if they don’t feel ready. Even when they do, sparring can be modified to match comfort and skill level.
Injury Reporting Culture: If someone feels discomfort or pain, they're encouraged to immediately stop and inform an instructor. There's no pressure to "tough it out."

**SafeSport Certification**: Instructors are SafeSport trained and certified, ensuring they understand and uphold national standards for creating a safe, respectful, and abuse-free training environment, especially for minors.

**What is the gym's policy on bullying or harassment?**
At Inverted Gear Academy, we are committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for all students, instructors, and visitors. Bullying, harassment, or any form of abusive behavior is strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated.

**Our Policy Includes:**
**Zero Tolerance for Bullying:**
This includes physical intimidation, verbal abuse, teasing, exclusion, or online harassment. Any behavior that intentionally harms or intimidates another person will be addressed immediately.

**Respect on and off the mats:**
We expect all members to treat each other with kindness and respect—whether in class, during open mats,
or on social media. That includes respecting personal boundaries, space, and communication styles.

**Harassment-Free Zone:**
Harassment—whether based on race, gender, sexual orientation, age, religion, or any other personal characteristic—has no place in our academy.

**Reporting and Confidentiality:**
If you experience or witness bullying or harassment, please report it to an instructor or staff member as soon as possible. All reports will be handled discreetly and respectfully.

**Disciplinary Action:** Depending on the severity of the incident, disciplinary action may include a warning, suspension, or permanent removal from the academy.
We are proud to foster a community where everyone can train, learn, and grow in a positive environment. Thank you for helping us make Inverted Gear Academy a safe space for all.
