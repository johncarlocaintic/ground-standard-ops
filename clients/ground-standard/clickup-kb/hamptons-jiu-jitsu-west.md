**General Directions:**
**Head toward Long Island:**
If you're coming from New York City or western Long Island, take I-495 East (Long Island Expressway) toward Riverhead. This is the most direct route into eastern Suffolk County.

**Look for Sunrise Highway (NY-27):**
From I-495, connect to NY-27 East via exit 70 (Manorville Rd). Follow NY-27 East for about 15–20 minutes. NY-27 is the main artery through the South Fork of Long Island.

**Nearby Landmarks:**
*   **Francis S. Gabreski Airport:** Located just north of Old Riverhead Rd, this small airport is a notable local landmark.
*   **Westhampton Beach High School:** Located on Lilac Rd, just off Old Riverhead Rd, and close to your destination.
*   **Westhampton Beach Village:** The central business district with shops, restaurants, and the Westhampton Beach Performing Arts Center.
*   **Southampton Police Substation:** Located nearby for local reference.
*   **Westhampton Dunes and Beaches:** Located just a few miles south, popular with both locals and tourists.

**Final Approach:**
As you travel on NY-27 East, take the exit for Old Riverhead Rd North (CR-31). Continue for about a mile. Look for **68 Old Riverhead Rd**, which should be along a stretch of mixed-use buildings and local businesses. The building may be located near service stations, office suites, or municipal facilities.
