**Class Information**
When a new person comes in, we recommend them to wear athletic attire and no loose-fitting clothes nor clothes with pockets or zippers as a safety precaution when rolling with other members. We ask for them to arrive 10-15 minutes early to fill out the waiver, meet instructors, and tour the facility for our amenities like water, towels, athlete's lounge, etc. We provide free water bottles and towels to all of our members and non-members. We encourage people to bring a water bottle, but if they forget or do not have one, we can provide the free water and towels. We do ask the lead if they have any experience in jiu jitsu or combat sports. With this, we can estimate how much we need to walk them through class and to what class we recommend their free trial class. For example, if they are a beginner, we would recommend for them to come to our 6:30 pm beginner class on Wednesday. If they ask what to expect from class, we tell them beginning of class we warm up, followed by Jiu Jitsu technique breakdowns and then live goal-oriented games based off the techniques of the day. For kids, if they ask what to expect from class, it's the same process but we do have a 2nd instructor that nwill walk them through the basic Day 1 fundamentals of jiu jitsu separate from the class

## **Additional Information**
We really emphasize for the kids that from 6-10 "that's a great age to start training" and flatter the parents by putting their kids into jiu jitsu/combat sports. We also tell them a lot of our kids are beginners, so their kids will not feel left out and behind. We offer multiple sales promotions throughout the year, but year- round ones that we can say are that we do offer family and sibling discounts.

For adults, we emphasize our high-level jiu jitsu and that all of the instructors have world-level competition experience. But that world-level standard does not stop at our instructors. We emphasize that our community is very engaging and that instructors put in the extra effort to connect and guide their students. They have dealt with all kinds of students with different goals prior: shy, experienced, looking for a community, looking for exercise and healthy lifestyle, so our instructors can navigate all kinds of athletes.

Our gym brand highlights making jiu jitsu more professional and feel luxurious. Our members should feel like they train at a higher-class gym with the all-white clean aesthetics, free waters, free customized gym towels, an athlete's lounge, etc., alongside exclusive community team events. We also host some of the highest class jiu jitsu competitors in the world (ADCC veterans) frequently due to our owner training at and competing for Simple Man Martial Arts (formerly B-Team) and being an instructor there prior to opening his own gym.
We also try to set ourselves apart from other gyms by encouraging our members to utilize our athlete's lounge to hang out and relax, especially on days where they feel their body's banged up but still want to come to the gym. We don't put down people who don't want to roll hard. Instead, we encourage our members to hang out in the lounge during class to watch film or watch the class instruction. For example, we have members who are injured still come to our gym and sit in the athlete's lounge to watch class and interact with the gym members who are participating in class.
