Bodega Jiu-Jitsu, founded in late 2022 by lifelong martial artists Kyvann Gonzalez and Chas Makk, was born out of a shared passion for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and a vision to create a unique training academy. As close friends and training partners, their shared dream of opening a school became reality when they realized their values and goals perfectly aligned.

Bodega Jiu-Jitsu stands out as a No-Gi only academy, offering cutting-edge instruction tailored to meet the needs of every practitioner—from seasoned competitors aiming to refine their skills to beginners looking to explore martial arts for the first time.  The founders believe martial arts are more than just a sport; they see it as a powerful tool for unlocking human potential and fostering personal growth.

Whether you're an experienced athlete or a hobbyist, Bodega Jiu-Jitsu offers a welcoming environment where you can challenge yourself, learn, and thrive. Join the journey and discover how martial arts can transform your life.
