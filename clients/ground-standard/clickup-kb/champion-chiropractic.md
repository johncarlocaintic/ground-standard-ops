## **Website "About Us" Section**
Champion Chiropractic of Pasadena, established in 2020, is a chiropractic wellness center in Pasadena, Texas, focused on helping patients heal better through non-invasive, evidence based therapies. The practice is led by Dr. Katina Jimenez, a 2016 graduate of Texas Chiropractic College, and aims to address pain at its source for a diverse patient base.

## **Website "Owner Biography" Section**
Dr. Katina Jimenez is a licensed Doctor of Chiropractic serving the Houston, Texas area. She is a 2016
graduate of Texas Chiropractic College and graduated as President of her class. She is a proud memberof the Texas Chiropractic Association and American Chiropractic Association. Dr. Jimenez holdscertification in Chiropractic Sports Medicine and is a USA Judo Certified Coach.

Dr. Jimenez works with patients of all ages and physical conditions. She knows the resolution to many
sources of pain should first be addressed with conservative care and not with addictive medications orexpensive surgeries. A personalized combination of chiropractic treatment, customized diets, physicaltherapy and targeted exercise gives patients their best chance for recovery. Dr. Jimenez is passionateabout health, nutrition and chiropractic care as an avenue for strengthening the body and promotinghealing. She knows all of these can effectively used to manage or eliminate pain, increase overall health and promote a body-positive lifestyle.

Dr. Jimenez is passionate about living a lifestyle that reflects her view of life. She is a competitive judoka, sand volleyball , equestrian and is happiest when learning something new. She is married to her high school sweetheart, Pedro Jimenez, and together they live with their two children on their farm in Alvin, Texas.
