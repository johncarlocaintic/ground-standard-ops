## **Kids Martial Arts**

**Age Range:** 5 to 12 
**Experience Requirement:** None
This class focuses on the fundamentals of exercise, basic martial art techniques, how to handle life issues appropriate for this age group, and, of course, fun!
**Do you need to be athletic to participate in martial arts?**
Not at all! You don’t need to be athletic to start learning martial arts. Our classes are designed to meet you where you are, whether you’re a complete beginner or already active. As you train, you’ll naturally build strength, coordination, flexibility, and endurance over time. Martial arts is about progress, not perfection—and anyone can benefit from the physical and mental growth it offers, regardless of your starting point. All you need is a willingness to learn and a positive attitude!
**Are these martial arts classes fun?**
Absolutely! Our martial arts classes are designed to be both fun and rewarding. While you’re learning powerful self-defense skills, improving your fitness, and building strength, flexibility, and coordination, you’re also sharpening your focus, boosting confidence, and relieving stress. Every class is structured to challenge you at your level in an engaging, supportive environment—so you’ll never feel bored or out of place. Whether you’re a child or an adult, you’ll leave each class feeling stronger, more focused, and excited to come back!
**What's a good age for a child to start martial arts?**
Honestly, practically every age is a good age to start! We have young children, as well as teenagers. We also have specific programs for adults. So no matter what age - we've got you covered.
**Can martial arts cause injury?**
Because we place such a huge focus on safety and injury prevention, we're proud to say that injuries are very few and far between with our program. In fact, because martial arts gets you in great shape, and makes you strong and flexible, people often see LESS injuries in their lives.
**Do you have to be in good shape to take martial arts?**
No, you don’t need to be in good shape to start learning martial arts. Our program is designed to help you get in shape while you train, regardless of your current fitness level. Classes are tailored to challenge you appropriately and safely, so you’ll build strength, endurance, and flexibility over time. Martial arts is a journey—everyone starts somewhere, and we’re here to support your progress every step of the way. All you need to begin is the desire to improve!
**Is martial arts violent?**
No! Because self-defense technique is taught in martial arts, it's often thought of as violent. However, the philosophies behind martial arts revolve around peace and harmony. The moves are more of a way to become connected with your body. We teach diplomacy and friendship. However, if a violent situation ever arises in your life, you'll have powerful tools to defend yourself and your loved ones.
