# Program Information:

We break down the classes in 3 levels.

All students new to BJJ start at the Fundamentals classes. Fundamental classes do not have sparring as part of the class. It consists of sequences of techniques, build in a comprehensive fashion progressively throughout the class. A class can have anywhere from 5 to 7 techniques offering the students the understanding of connection between one technique and the next. A quick warm with Jiu Jitsu specific movements is done on the first 5-10 min. (Rarely it will involve regular type of exercises like push ups etc. All Jiu Jitsu focused techniques. The new students are paired up with a more seasoned students on that class.

Fundamentals classes follow a curriculum of techniques and concepts that are foundational to BJJ and are the building blocks for the more advanced classes.

Intermediate and Advanced classes, follow a theme/framework where students work on the entire week. Not necessarily the exact same move or technique but techniques stem for the same initial position and concept, incorporating possible reactions and alternative routes, chaining and connecting moves together. They are basically the same class, we just differentiate in terms of who can or cannot attend them.

For students with some experience (white belt 1 stripe) they can also come to Intermediate classes, where the format includes warm up, technique drilling and sparring.

For students (white belt 2 stripes and above) they can also come to Advanced classes.

If a student is brand new to BJJ, it takes an average of 30 classes to be able to also come to Intermediate classes. (Just an average so students can track progression. Some students might be 15 classes, some might be 40...etc)

Trial classes are free and for people that live in NYC are actively looking for a school to join. We generally don't do pure visitor drop ins due to space limitation and safety. Loaner gi, rashguard and belts are offered for trials so they don't need their own gi. Leads should plan to arrive 10-15 prior to class so they can sign the waiver and get ready for class. A bottle of water is a good idea but we do have bottles of water for sale if needed.

For kids. 5-8 Little Warriors. 9-13 Teenage Warriors. The transition from one class to the other depends on size and maturity, not belt level. At 13 a child might start transitioning to adults fundamentals classes depending on size and experience. At 14, they are fully in the adults classes.
