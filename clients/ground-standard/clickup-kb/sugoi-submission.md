## Class Information
### Sugoi Submissions – Intro Class (First Visit) Full Breakdown
### 1) What an intro class is (what they’ll actually do)
Your first class is designed to be safe, structured, and beginner-friendly — even if you’ve never trained before.
*   Typical flow (60 minutes):
*   Welcome + quick check-in (2–3 min)
*   We’ll confirm your name, goals, any injuries, and answer quick questions.
*   Warm-up (8–12 min)
*   Light movement + basic body mechanics (nothing crazy).
*   Technique block (15–20 min)
*   We teach 1–2 core fundamentals with clear steps and coaching.
*   Guided drilling (10–15 min)
*   You’ll practice with a partner while coaches walk the room and help.
*   Optional live rounds (10–15 min)
### If it’s your first day, you can:
*   sit out and watch, or
*   do light positional sparring (controlled), or
*   roll if you feel comfortable and the coach gives the green light
*   No one gets thrown into the deep end.
*   Wrap-up (2–5 min)
*   We’ll answer questions, show next steps, and talk schedule/membership options.
### 2) What to wear (uniform requirements)
This depends on the class type:
**For a Gi Jiu-Jitsu class**
*   Best option: Gi (kimono) + belt if you have one
If you don’t have a gi yet: you can still come in for your first class.
*   Wear a t-shirt or rashguard + athletic shorts with no pockets or zippers (or leggings/spats).
We’ll guide you on getting a gi after you try it.

**For No-Gi / Wrestling**
*   Rashguard or fitted athletic shirt
*   Shorts with no pockets/zipper or spats/leggings
*   Wrestling shoes are optional if it’s a wrestling class (see below).

**What NOT to wear**
*   Jeans, cargo shorts, shorts with zippers, anything with metal
*   Jewelry (rings, bracelets, necklaces, watches) — please remove before stepping on the mat

### **3) Equipment needed (what to bring)**
**Required for first class:**
*   Water bottle (highly recommended)
*   Small towel if you sweat a lot
*   Flip flops/slides for walking off the mat (helps keep the mats clean)
*   Optional / helpful:
*   Mouthguard (recommended if you plan to spar regularly)
*   Knee pads (some people like them, especially for wrestling)
**We do not require you to buy anything before trying a class.**

### 4) Arrival time (this matters)
Be here 15–20 minutes early for your first visit.
**That gives time for:**
*   quick intro + waiver
*   a tour / where to put your stuff
*   hygiene + safety rules
*   pairing you with the right training partner
*   If it’s not your first visit, 5–10 minutes early is perfect.

### 5) Hygiene & safety rules (non-negotiables)
**This is a family academy — we keep it clean and safe.**
**Before class:**
*   Trim nails (hands + feet)
*   Shower / deodorant (please)
*   Clean training clothes (fresh shirt/gi every session)
*   On the mat
*   No shoes on the mat
*   Use footwear when walking off the mat (bathroom = always wear footwear)
*   Tell the coach about injuries before class starts
*   Tap early, tap often — safety first
*   If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or hurt: stop and tell a coach immediately

### 6) Do they need to be in shape first?
No. Training is how you get in shape. We’ll scale the pace and intensity. You can take breaks anytime.

### 7) Can they bring kids / watch?
Parents are typically welcome to watch kids class (as long as it doesn’t distract the room).
For adult class, we can be flexible, but we want the environment to stay focused and safe.

### 8) After class: what happens next (simple and clear)
After your intro class we’ll:
*   ask what you thought
*   recommend which classes match your goals (Gi, No-Gi, beginners, wrestling, etc.)
*   go over membership options and the best next step
