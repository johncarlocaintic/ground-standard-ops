### Class Information
We recommend our 'Basics' class for students who have little to no experience with BJJ. Our team is extremely welcoming to new students and beginners - we all had a first day on the mats. Uniform rentals are available but on a first come first serve - so we recommend that they schedule their initial trial class (which has previously done by emailing us or by phone) and we can guesstimate their gi size using their height + t-shirt size. We recommend bringing in shoes they can easily slide on and off (they will be barefoot on the mats but we require shoes everywhere else, especially in the bathrooms), and we also recommend bringing a water bottle although water is always available. Nails must be neatly trimmed due to the nature of the sport. Students should expect to remove jewelry throughout the class. Minors must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at the first class for the waiver, but this can also be accomplished by email if needed (i.e. a 17 y/o who can drive themselves). No pressure to participate in sparring especially in the first classes. We want new students to do their best, ask questions, and most importantly have fun. We're happy to help make their first experience in jiu jitsu and/or at Breathe an enjoyable one. We always express gratitude for their interest in Breathe Jiu Jitsu as well whenever scheduling a free trial or answering an inquiry email.

Basics is designed with new students in mind, but is comprised of technical details that benefit students whether they have trained for 10 days or 10 years. We often have blue + purple belts in this class. Classes begin with a warm-up, drilling (3-4 techniques are taught and broken down by detailed instruction, followed by time to work on the drill with a partner), specific training/live training putting those drills into practice, and sparring. Classes are 60 mins long.

Beginners can join any of the kids' classes. If a student is between the age of 6 to 7 we determine whether they belong to the Little Kids program vs the Big Kids program on a case by case basis. Free trials consist of (3) free classes that expire 1 week after the initial visit (for the Little Kids Program, families have 2 weeks after the initial visit to use free trial classes).

### Additional Information
We try to be very encouraging of new students, whatever their goal is through jiu jitsu or whatever is driving them to start training. We all know what it's like to have a first day on the mats and we want to encourage students to pursue jiu jitsu because of it's ability to have an big impact on overall quality of life. Students who train spend a significant amount of time in their home academy, and we want Breathe to feel like another home for them.

On another note, while we may not make this part of our 'pitch', we don't sugar coat how tough jiu jitsu can be. But the long term benefits far outweigh the hardships or obstacles of starting something new. We 3 really believe in the discipline the practice builds, the community it brings, and the way that the lessons begin to impact other areas of one's life both on and off the mats to improve overall quality of life. This is a tone we carry when speaking to new inquiries; we are very motivating and we relate to students because we are them.

Respect can ALWAYS be expected when they walk through our doors, and at the same time, a lack of respect will not be tolerated. Again, this is not something that is part of my 'pitch' but I have had a small handful of inquiries who come at me pretty disrespectfully, and while I maintain my calm disposition, I really don't spend much time trying to get them to schedule a trial.

Danny comes from a background of full time competing nationally and internationally under AOJ/Mendes Bros so he has experience both as a competitor himself as well as coaching competitors. He was an instructor and competitor under AOJ between 2012 - 2018, and he is one of the Mendes Bros first black belts. Jamie solely trains for the lifestyle benefits and is currently a purple belt under the Mendes Bros. I mention this to say that we recognize everyone has different goals through jiu jitsu and we're happy to help where we can. We focus on building a program that is family friendly but also harbors an environment encouraging hard work, surpassing self-limiting beliefs, and grit

We advertise our free trial as one use per Suffolk Count resident. However, if a student uses one free trial class and never comes back, we are happy to honor another free trial period if they return within 6 months. If a student is visiting from a school that is owned by a Mendes Bros black belt, we honor their first visit.

We offer a small membership discount to military personnel, veterans, law enforcement officers, and emergency responders. Discount is applied to their membership with valid ID, and is extended to immediate family (spouses + children).

Our focus with the kids aside from developing jiu jitsu skills is to teach discipline, emotional regulation (all feelings are welcome, all behaviors are not), build confidence, develop patience with themselves and their training partners and breed respectful attitudes. We aim to teach qualities of young leaders using jiu jitsu techniques as our modality.
