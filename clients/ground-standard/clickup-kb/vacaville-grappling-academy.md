**Do you allow drop-ins for classes?**
Yes, $40 per session

**What types of martial arts classes do you offer?**
Grappling classes - Jiu-jitsu and wrestling

**How do I sign up for a class?**
You can sign up online at [https://apexgrapplingvacaville.com/signup](https://apexgrapplingvacaville.com/signup) or sign up at the gym

**Are there any trial classes available for new students?**
Yes, one-day free trial at any class offered on the schedule

**What is the schedule for beginner classes?**
The beginners and all level classes are together.

**Do I need to bring any equipment for my first class?**
Please wear a rash guard or well-fitted shirt, pocketless shorts, and bring a water container. Shoes are
required off the mats. Towels and mouthguards are optional.

**Can I watch a class before I decide to join?**
Yes! You can come and watch any practice on the schedule.

**Do you offer family discounts?**
Yes, we offer a monthly membership for three family members at $400.

**Is there a uniform required, and where can I purchase it?**
Uniforms are not required, but you can purchase our rash guards and shorts at the online store or the gym.

**What should I wear to my first martial arts class?**
Rash guard or well-fitted shirt and pocketless shorts.

**Do you have locker room facilities?**
We have cubbies for personal storage and a single bathroom.

**Are there classes specifically for women?**
No classes specifically for women. We make all classes inclusive

**Is there parking available at the facility?**
Yes, we have a parking lot in front of the gym, street parking, and overflow parking.

**What safety measures are in place during training?**
I am an EMT, BLS provider, and have medical equipment on stand-by for medical emergencies. I have fire equipment for fires and an emergency plan for natural disasters.

**Do you offer private lessons?**
I do offer private lessons for 30 min at $50

**Can I pause my membership for a vacation or other reasons?**
Yes, if you need to pause your membership, please notify me as soon as possible.

**Is there a cancellation fee for memberships?**
There are no cancellation fees.

**What is the student-to-instructor ratio in your classes?**
The ratio ranges from 1 coach to 6 - 20 students

**Can parents watch their children during classes?**
Yes, but with limited space, we encourage parents to drop off their kids.

**How often do you test for belt promotions?**
We do not test for belt promotions. Students are promoted based on three criteria:
1\. How consistently they train.
2\. How competitive they are with their peers on the competition mat or practice mat.
3\. Are their skills in the standing, guard, and pinning situations improving?

**What forms of payment do you accept?**
We accept cash and card payments

**Are there any additional fees apart from the membership cost?**
There is a one-time $50 sign-up fee.

**What is the best way to contact the gym outside of business hours?**
You can email at [apexgrapplingvacaville@gmail.com](mailto:apexgrapplingvacaville@gmail.com) or text at 707-676-3098

**Are there any special offers for new members?**
No special offers

**What is the gym's policy on bullying or harassment?**
We do not tolerate bullying or harassment. The coach will immediately address the situation and will
investigate further if needed. Once the coach has all the information, actions will be taken based on the investigation.

**Are there any community events or outreach programs?**
We are partnered with the City of Vacaville for our Youth Program. You can use Measure M funds through the City if applicable.

**Can I bring a guest to the gym?**
You can bring a guest, and we encourage it!

**What are the cleanliness and hygiene protocols at the gym?**
We clean before and after each class. First, we vacuum the mats for dirt, dust, and hair. We spray the wall and floor mats with a disinfectant, deodorizer, fungicide, and virucide to clean the mats. All other areas are vacuumed and cleaned with disinfectant.

**Are there any online classes or resources available?**
No online sources available at this time.

**Do you offer discounts for military, first responders, or students?**
Yes, 15% off your monthly membership.

**Are there specific classes focused on self-defense?**
There are no classes titled "self-defense," but at every practice, we practice the standing situation, which includes hand fighting and being able to stand up while being held down. These are imperative skills to know in self-defense.

**Can members bring their own equipment, or is it provided?**
Yes. The only equipment needed is the proper attire and a water container.

**What is your gym's policy on respectful conduct and sportsmanship?**
We pride ourselves on the culture and atmosphere we have created on the mat. This includes respecting others at all times and encouraging learning skills over "winning." No one cares if you "win." We do care about having fun and respecting others.

**What are the qualifications and backgrounds of your instructors?**
We have one coach and owner.
Coach Nick and his family have been part of the Vacaville community since 2009. He is a U.S. Air Force veteran with a deep passion for health and physical fitness.
He began training Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (BJJ) in 2018 and instantly fell in love with the sport. He has attended seminars, specifically in coaching and practice design, and trained at multiple gyms throughout California. He has competed in multiple sports, including boxing, football, and BJJ. He maintains his medical training such as EMT, CPR, and AED. He uses his Protective Security training and career experience to infuse self-defense skills into his practices. His coaching and training began under Professor Cleidson Ramos of Apex BJJ Academy Vacaville in 2020.
