### Class Information
We ask appointments arrive about 15 minutes prior to the class start. I typically make the appointment slots 15 minutes prior automatically. If the class is at 6pm for example, the appointment slot would be scheduled for 5:45 and the emails tell them that. This DOES sometimes create an issue where someone shows up at 5:30 because THEY want to be early not having read that it is already scheduled early. Open to discussion on this. We do provide a loaner uniform currently, although I am honestly getting tired of washing uniforms when people don't enroll. Also open to discuss. We do suggest they bring a water bottle, but we do have a water machine available with paper cups. I include the waiver as part of the appointment setting process and do ask them to sign it in email and text prior to the appointment, but many peopled don't so we leave time for that. They will be jumping directly into their first class that day. It is important they arrive on time if we set it early, or arrive early so they can get the uniform, etc.
