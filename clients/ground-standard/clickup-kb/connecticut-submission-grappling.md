# **Details:**
We offer 100% live training geared towards no-gi jiu jitsu only. We do not offer any other type of martial arts training. All classes are 100% live training scaled for the experience level of the athletes. We tailor the practice based upon the behaviors we see in the room each and every class. We offer community open mats on the weekend as well as free female training on Fridays during the Womens only class. The open mats not only bring new people to the gym, they also add variety to our students sparring rounds weekly.

Our foundations class is ideal for new grapplers up to two years of experience. It consists of on average six 6 minute live games focusing on two positions, usually a guarded and a pinning position with low variability. In our all levels class class, we will do anywhere from 5-10 minute rounds with higher variability. We will introduce standing engagement. This class is recommended for more experienced grapplers. Trial students are welcome as long as they have some previous experience. Leads should arrive at least 15 minutes early to sign the waiver and get acquainted with the coaches and space. Personal water bottles are preferable but we do have a water fountain. No gym uniforms required, shorts and t shirt or rash guard are sufficient. Shorts should not have pockets or any metal fixtures. No jewelry. Fingernails should be trimmed and filed. Open cuts or scrapes should be covered prior to class.
