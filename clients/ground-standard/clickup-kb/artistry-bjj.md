### Website "About Us" Section
Artistry BJJ is a family-oriented Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy dedicated to helping kids and adults grow stronger, more confident, and more disciplined both on and off the mat. We provide high-quality instruction in a safe, respectful, and supportive environment where students of all experience levels can learn, train, and improve at their own pace. Our focus goes beyond techniques and competition we emphasize character, community, and personal growth, creating a positive culture where families feel welcome and students build skills that last a lifetime.
### Website "Owner Biography" Section
Felipe Rocha is the owner and head instructor of Artistry BJJ. With nearly two decades of experience in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Felipe is passionate about teaching effective, technical jiu-jitsu in a safe, family oriented environment. As both a competitor and coach, he brings a deep understanding of the art and discipline of BJJ while emphasizing character, confidence, and personal growth. Felipe is committed to building a welcoming academy where kids and adults alike can develop skills that extend far beyond the mat.

### Business Mission Statement
Artistry BJJ is a family-oriented academy dedicated to building confidence, discipline, and resilience through Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in a safe, respectful, and supportive environment for all ages.
