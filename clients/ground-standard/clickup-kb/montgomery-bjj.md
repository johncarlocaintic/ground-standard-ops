**Website "About Us" Section**
We have been serving the Montgomery Twp. and Sommerset County community since 2017. Through
Brazilian Jiu Jitsu we teach adukts and children the importance of self defense and "bully-proofing" . We have served hundreds of residents and strive to make an impact on not just the individual, but the community as well.

**Website "Owner Biography" Section**
Miguel Benitez is a third degree black belt under Tom DeBlass. He began training Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in
2007 and since then has competed and coached in various rules sets and events. He brings nearly 20
years of jiu jitsu and coaching experience to the mats every single night and is eager to share all of his knowledge. Recently Miguel was named Head Referee for ADCC America and has officiated for ONE FC in Thailand.
