**Do you allow drop-ins for classes?**

Visitors from out of town don’t pay unless they’re going to be in the area frequently. We offer the people in the middle (around temporarily or are in the area semi-frequently) a $200

**What types of martial arts classes do you offer?**
Grappling (BJJ/judo/wrestling) in the gi and out of the gi depending on the schedule.
We also offer striking privates (kickboxing/Muay Thai).

**How do I sign up for a class?**
New / returning (alum) students:
Existing members do NOT need to book but we encourage them to download the zenplanner app and book their class in advance to make attendance quicker.

**Are there any trial classes available for new students?**
They all work into existing classes (ANY class will be fine for a new student, though some classes are
more optimal - like Tues/thurs @6am or Mon/wed @7pm for adults). We offer a free private lesson when appropriate/as needed.

**What is the schedule for beginner classes?**
6am Tues/thurs adults
7pm Mon/wed adults
Kids don’t have fundamentals classes, they are grouped with specific coaches for “more” fundamentals work.

**Do I need to bring any equipment for my first class?**
Just proper gym attire - no pockets, zippers or buttons.

**Can I watch a class before I decide to join?**
Sure!

**Do you offer family discounts?**
Yes. $20 off membership price for each additional member (it does NOT stack, it’s just $20 off the chosen tier for each additional member).

**Is there a uniform required, and where can I purchase it?**
Yes for gi classes - we give ALL new members a new gi when they sign up.
No gi - whatever works, just no pockets, zippers or buttons.

**What should I wear to my first martial arts class?**
T shirt or rashguard and shorts with no pockets or zippers or buttons. Leggings are fine too.

**Do you have locker room facilities?**
No but we have bathrooms AND changing stalls.

**Are there classes specifically for women?**
No, we philosophically disagree with that approach for self-defense reasons.

**Is there parking available at the facility?**
Yes! Tons!

**What safety measures are in place during training?**
Floating floors, attention to detail, because we use the constraints-led approach coaches can focus on the entire room instead of 1 group at a time.
Students are told when to ramp intensity up or down. Nonsense is not tolerated. Students are taught early on to communicate with their partner to avoid safety issues.

**Do you offer private lessons?**
Yes.

**Can I pause my membership for a vacation or other reasons?**
Yes. Up to 3 months. We need a start and end date whenever someone pauses.

**Is there a cancellation fee for memberships?**
No, only when they use the lovely new click to cancel thanks to the ftc. If they call/email us there is NEVER a fee.

**What is the student-to-instructor ratio in your classes?**
Varies. With the kids 5 or 10 to 1. For adults, approx 10 to 1 but can be bigger.

**Can parents watch their children during classes?**
Yes. But they need to be quiet and coaching is NOT tolerated.

**How often do you test for belt promotions?**
Zero. You are GIVEN your stripe or belt and certificate at the end of class when you are ready.

We’re all belted under Charles & Ralph Gracie: self-defense is ALWAYS factored into our material in some fashion, sometimes more sometimes less - it depends on the material we are covering/whether the team is prepping for a tournament (and the subsequent ruleset we’ll be dealing with).

**Can members bring their own equipment, or is it provided?**
Bring your own.

**What is your gym's policy on respectful conduct and sportsmanship?**
1 gentle-ish warning, at most a handful of non-gentle reminders after…possible cancellation of
membership once a pattern is made clear.

**What are the qualifications and backgrounds of your instructors?**
Brown & black belts under Charles & Ralph Gracie.
Some have been grappling for over 20 years. Several high-level wrestlers. One was a strength and
conditioning coach for the SF 49ers. Another an army ranger. Another teaches defense tactics for the
Sacramento police department.
Our website outlines our instructors’ backgrounds thoroughly on the home page.

**Do you have Wi-Fi available in the gym?**
Yes and no.
If you’re a coach/volunteer or a student/family member of a student with a work or school emergency -temporary access can be granted. We have too many students to accommodate this all the time.

**Can I host a birthday party or private event at the gym?**
Yes, we are open to private events but it depends on what the event is (we get logical requests that we’re open to, like wrestling camps, and insane requests, like line dancing where they want to do some kind of dance party and want to wear shoes on the mats).

**Are there any specific rules regarding photography or filming in the gym?**
No issue - we have common sense rules on this: don’t intentionally film other people’s kids without
consent, that’s both inappropriate and weird, and if filming your child…please stay in your seat - walking around the mat area to do this is distracting to the kids and won’t be tolerated. Filming yourself (adults) and your rounds is absolutely fine.

**How is progress and attendance monitored for children's classes?**
Attendance via zenplanner. Every 25 sessions a child is eligible for a stripe - they get it if they’re meeting behavioral and performance goals in that period. No child that has a meltdown during that class will receive a stripe or the belt they’re due for, they’ll have to wait until their next class and perform.

**Can I get a tour of the gym before deciding to join?**
Sure. We always do this when someone comes in for the first time. We don’t do formal tours outside of class hours unless a walk-in happens to catch us just before or just after classes.

**How do you accommodate members with special needs or disabilities?**
Yes. We have kiddos with all kinds of special needs. Adults are accommodated to the degree that is
possible - we would never turn someone away unless there was a behavioral issue at play (example: group classes will not work for a kid that has an issue that cause them to scream constantly, or is completely incapable of following basic instructions, or an adult that cannot comprehend other peoples boundaries). I hope this makes sense.

**How do you accommodate members with allergies? Are there precautions in place incase of an**
**emergency?**
This has never been an issue. We have a well stocked first aid kit and an AED. If a student or a parent of a student wants us to store their epipen in our first aid kit, that’s fine. We’re in California so we can’t get them ourselves. We also have Narcan and Benadryl in the kit.
