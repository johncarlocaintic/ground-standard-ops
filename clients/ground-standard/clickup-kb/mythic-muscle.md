### Do I need to bring any equipment for my first class?
For your first class be sure to bring some water or electrolytes beverage of choice, wear some athletic
shorts preferably with no pockets and zippers, and a shirt that is not too loose.
### Can I watch a class before I decide to join?
Yes, beginners are more than welcome to watch a class before stepping in to determine if this is
something they would like to try.
